SCALE EXERCISES - SECTION III, LEVEL 21
NATURAL MINOR ONE-FRET NECK LADDER: A TO A

PURPOSE
Learn one compact one-octave natural-minor shape, move the complete hand-and-thumb frame exactly one fret,
and repeat through every natural-minor key. Begin with A natural minor at low-E fret 5, climb to A natural
minor at fret 17, then reverse and return one fret at a time.

PARALLEL MAJOR VS NATURAL MINOR
A major:         A B C# D E F# G# A
A natural minor: A B C  D E F  G  A
The 3rd, 6th, and 7th are each lowered one fret: 3 -> b3, 6 -> b6, 7 -> b7.

NATURAL-MINOR FORMULA
Degrees:   1 2 b3 4 5 b6 b7 8
Intervals: whole - half - whole - whole - half - whole - whole

COMPACT ONE-OCTAVE SHAPE - ROOT FRET R ON LOW E
Low E string: R, R+2, R+3          Fingers 1,3,4
A string:     R, R+2, R+3          Fingers 1,3,4
D string:     R, R+2               Fingers 1,3
The four-fret hand frame runs from R through R+3.
Ascending notes per string: 3-3-2. Descending notes per string: 2-3-3.

CELL AND PICKING RULE
Play degrees 1-2-b3-4-5-b6-b7-8, then 8-b7-b6-5-4-b3-2-1. The octave root is repeated at the turn.
This creates 16 notes, so the next key begins with the same alternate-picking orientation.
D-start: D U D U D U D U | D U D U D U D U
U-start: U D U D U D U D | U D U D U D U D
Do not restart the pick cycle at the scale turn or at the one-fret shift.

NECK ROUTE
UP:   A(5) > Bb(6) > B(7) > C(8) > C#(9) > D(10) > Eb(11) > E(12) > F(13) > F#(14) > G(15) > G#(16) > A(17)
DOWN: A(17) > G#(16) > G(15) > F#(14) > F(13) > E(12) > Eb(11) > D(10) > C#(9) > C(8) > B(7) > Bb(6) > A(5)

PRACTICE RULE
First compare A major and A natural minor and identify the lowered 3rd, 6th, and 7th. Then use 3-4
neighboring natural-minor rungs. Play even eighth notes and pause one beat after each 16-note cell.
During the pause, release pressure and move the complete frame one fret. Repeat with both pick starts.
Then remove the pause, connect the ladder, reverse at fret 17, and return to fret 5.
Add 2 BPM after three clean round trips. Reduce 4-6 BPM after two errors, dragged shift noise, or rising tension.
Advance after the complete route is clean with both pick starts on two separate days.

FULL ROUND-TRIP CHALLENGE
1. Start with A natural minor at fret 5.
2. Play all 13 cells without restarting the alternate-picking cycle.
3. Reverse at fret 17 and return through rungs 12-01.
4. Name each key and say the root before its first note.
5. Repeat with the opposite starting stroke.
One complete route contains 25 cells x 16 notes = 400 picked notes.

CLEAN-ROUND STANDARD
No open strings, no dragged shift noise, no missing accidental, no hesitation at the 3-3-2 crossings,
and no tempo change at the one-fret relocations.

AFTER THE BASIC LADDER IS CLEAN
Parallel contrast: play one A-major cell, then one A-natural-minor cell; hear and locate b3, b6, and b7.
Accent rotation: accent degrees 1, then b3, then 5, then b7 without changing the fingering.
Rhythm: eighth notes, straight sixteenths, then four-note bursts with one beat of rest between keys.
Direction: begin some sessions at fret 17 and descend first.
Random entry: choose any root fret from 5-17, name the minor key, play its cell, then continue either way.

MASTERY GATE
Complete the full route with both pick starts on two separate days. Name every key, preserve the minor-scale
formula, keep equal note length and volume, and recover the correct key after a deliberate random stop.
