SCALE EXERCISES - SECTION II, LEVEL 12
MAJOR SCALE ONE-FRET NECK LADDER: A TO A

PURPOSE
Play one compact one-octave major-scale shape, move the complete hand-and-thumb frame exactly one fret,
and repeat through every major key. Begin with A major at low-E fret 5, climb to A major at fret 17,
then reverse and return one fret at a time.

IMPORTANT INTERPRETATION
Moving one fixed scale shape by one fret transposes it. A major is the starting rung, not the key of every rung.
A fixed-key A-major exercise in every neck position requires changing the note layout and is a separate later branch.

MAJOR-SCALE FORMULA
Degrees: 1 2 3 4 5 6 7 8
Intervals: whole - whole - half - whole - whole - whole - half

COMPACT ONE-OCTAVE SHAPE - ROOT FRET R ON LOW E
Low E string: R, R+2               Fingers 2,4
A string:     R-1, R, R+2          Fingers 1,2,4
D string:     R-1, R+1, R+2        Fingers 1,3,4
The four-fret hand frame runs from R-1 through R+2.

CELL AND PICKING RULE
Play degrees 1-2-3-4-5-6-7-8, then 8-7-6-5-4-3-2-1. The octave root is repeated at the turn.
This creates 16 notes, so the next key begins with the same alternate-picking orientation.
D-start: D U D U D U D U | D U D U D U D U
U-start: U D U D U D U D | U D U D U D U D
Do not restart the pick cycle at the scale turn or at the one-fret shift.

NECK ROUTE
UP:   A(5) > Bb(6) > B(7) > C(8) > Db(9) > D(10) > Eb(11) > E(12) > F(13) > Gb(14) > G(15) > Ab(16) > A(17)
DOWN: A(17) > Ab(16) > G(15) > Gb(14) > F(13) > E(12) > Eb(11) > D(10) > Db(9) > C(8) > B(7) > Bb(6) > A(5)

PRACTICE RULE
Use 3-4 neighboring rungs at first. Play even eighth notes and pause one beat after every 16-note cell.
During the pause, release pressure and move the complete frame one fret. Repeat with both pick starts.
Then remove the pause, connect the ladder, reverse at fret 17, and return to fret 5.
Add 2 BPM after three clean round trips. Reduce 4-6 BPM after two errors, dragged shift noise, or rising tension.
Advance after the complete route is clean with both pick starts on two separate days.

FULL ROUND-TRIP CHALLENGE
Start at fret 5, play rungs 01-13 without a pick reset, reverse, and return through rungs 12-01.
Say each key name aloud before its root. Repeat with the opposite starting stroke.
Clean standard: no open strings, no dragged shift noise, no wrong accidental, and no tempo change at relocations.

AFTER MASTERY
Rotate accents through scale degrees 1, 3, 5, and 7; begin at fret 17 and descend first; or enter at a random root fret,
name the key, play the cell, and continue in either direction. These are variants of Level 12, not separate full levels.
