SCALE EXERCISES - SECTION II, LEVEL 19
MAJOR SCALE DIATONIC TRIADS - TWO-OCTAVE SIX-STRING NECK LADDER
==========================================================================================================================

PURPOSE
Level 19 stacks one more scale tone onto the diatonic-thirds idea from Level 18. Every group now plays
a root, third, and fifth: 1-3-5, 2-4-6, 3-5-7, and onward. The exercise converts the major scale into
a connected chain of broken triads while retaining the same two-octave six-string shape and one-fret route.

MAIN SEQUENCE
Ascending:  1-3-5 | 2-4-6 | 3-5-7 | 4-6-8 | 5-7-9 | 6-8-10 | 7-9-11 | 8-10-12 | 9-11-13 | 10-12-14 | 11-13-15
Descending: 15-13-11 | 14-12-10 | 13-11-9 | 12-10-8 | 11-9-7 | 10-8-6 | 9-7-5 | 8-6-4 | 7-5-3 | 6-4-2 | 5-3-1

HARMONIC FUNCTION AND QUALITY
Ascending:  I major | ii minor | iii minor | IV major | V major | vi minor | vii diminished | I major | ii minor | iii minor | IV major
Descending: IV major | iii minor | ii minor | I major | vii diminished | vi minor | V major | IV major | iii minor | ii minor | I major

CELL DESIGN
- 11 ascending triads / 33 notes
- repeat the high root at the turnaround as the first note of the descending line
- 11 descending triads / 33 notes
- 22 triads / 66 notes total per key
- shift the entire hand-and-thumb frame one fret only after the final 5-3-1 triad

PICKING
Downstroke start: D-U-D | U-D-U | D-U-D | U-D-U ... through all 22 triads.
Upstroke start:   U-D-U | D-U-D | U-D-U | D-U-D ... through all 22 triads.
Every three-note triad flips the starting stroke of the next triad. The complete 66-note cell is even,
so every new key begins on the same pick orientation. Do not reset at group boundaries, string changes,
the middle octave, the repeated high root, or a new key.

RHYTHM
Learning pulse: one triad per beat as eighth-note triplets.
Performance pulse: two triads per beat as sixteenth-note triplets (sextuplets).
Accent the root of each triad while learning, then remove the accents and equalize every note.

PRACTICE PROGRESSION
1. A major, first octave only: 1-3-5 through 4-6-8, then reverse.
2. A major, all 11 ascending triads.
3. A major, all 11 descending triads.
4. Link the 66-note cell; articulate both high-root notes without resetting.
5. Use two or three neighboring keys with a one-beat pause only after each complete cell.
6. Remove the pause and complete the entire route with both pick starts.

CHROMATIC ROUTE
Up:   A -> Bb -> B -> C -> Db -> D -> Eb -> E -> F -> Gb -> G -> Ab -> A
Down: Ab -> G -> Gb -> F -> E -> Eb -> D -> Db -> C -> B -> Bb -> A
Do not replay the high A-major rung during the down pass unless beginning a new round.

Tempo rule: add 2 BPM after three clean trips; reduce 4-6 BPM after two errors or signs of tension.

CLEAN-ROUND STANDARD
- no missing, doubled, reversed, or reordered triad
- every group preserves the root-third-fifth spacing and one-degree root motion
- correct string changes, B-string offset, and assigned fingering
- both high-root notes at the turn are clearly articulated and equal in volume
- no scrape or tempo change during a one-fret shift
- major, minor, and diminished triad qualities remain audible inside one connected line

VARIATIONS AFTER THE BASIC LADDER IS CLEAN
1. Accent the root of every triad, then remove all triad accents.
2. Accent only the fifth of every triad without rushing into the next root.
3. Say the Roman numeral before each triad in one selected key.
4. Name the actual chords in one selected key.
5. Play one triad per beat, then two triads per beat as sextuplets at the same metronome marking.
6. Begin selected sessions at fret 17 and descend first, or choose a random root fret from 5-17.
