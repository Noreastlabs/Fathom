SCALE EXERCISES - SECTION III, LEVEL 27
NATURAL MINOR DIATONIC THIRDS - TWO-OCTAVE SIX-STRING NECK LADDER
======================================================================================================================================================

PURPOSE
Level 27 leaves stepwise and rolling natural-minor motion and begins intervallic sequencing.
Each pair skips one scale degree: 1-b3, 2-4, b3-5, and onward. After each upper note,
the next pair rolls back one degree and continues upward. The exercise retains the same
two-octave six-string natural-minor shape and one-fret chromatic neck route used in Levels
22, 24, and 26.

WHAT CHANGES FROM LEVEL 26
Level 26 moved in overlapping four-note scale fragments. Level 27 replaces those fragments
with two-note diatonic thirds. The mechanical demand is shorter, but the musical demand is
higher: hear whether each pair is a minor third or major third, preserve the one-degree
rollback, and keep every string crossing inside one uninterrupted line.

NATURAL-MINOR FORMULA
One octave: 1 2 b3 4 5 b6 b7 8
Two octaves: 1 2 b3 4 5 b6 b7 8 9 b10 11 12 b13 b14 15
Intervals: whole - half - whole - whole - half - whole - whole, then repeat

MAIN SEQUENCE
Ascending:  1-b3 | 2-4 | b3-5 | 4-b6 | 5-b7 | b6-8 | b7-9 | 8-b10 | 9-11 | b10-12 | 11-b13 | 12-b14 | b13-15
Descending: 15-b13 | b14-12 | b13-11 | 12-b10 | 11-9 | b10-8 | 9-b7 | 8-b6 | b7-5 | b6-4 | 5-b3 | 4-2 | b3-1
Each new pair begins one scale position farther forward. Between pairs, the line rolls back
one degree: 1-b3 then 2-4; 4-b6 then 5-b7. Do not pause or reset at the rollback.

INTERVAL-QUALITY PATTERN
Ascending:  m3 | m3 | M3 | m3 | m3 | M3 | M3 | m3 | m3 | M3 | m3 | m3 | M3
Descending: M3 | m3 | m3 | M3 | m3 | m3 | M3 | M3 | m3 | m3 | M3 | m3 | m3
M3 = major third = 4 semitones. m3 = minor third = 3 semitones.
First-octave natural-minor pattern: m3 | m3 | M3 | m3 | m3 | M3 | M3.
Natural minor uses the same two third sizes as major, but the order begins differently.

TWO-OCTAVE SIX-STRING SHAPE - ROOT FRET R ON LOW E
Low E string: R, R+2, R+3          Fingers 1,3,4        Positions 1,2,b3
A string:     R, R+2, R+3          Fingers 1,3,4        Positions 4,5,b6
D string:     R, R+2               Fingers 1,3          Positions b7,8
G string:     R-1, R, R+2          Fingers 1,2,4        Positions 9,b10,11
B string:     R, R+1, R+3          Fingers 1,2,4        Positions 12,b13,b14
High e:       R                    Finger 1             Position 15
The complete map spans R-1 through R+3. Release and rebuild for the G-string backreach and
again for the B-string offset; do not hold a static five-fret stretch.

STRING-PATH MAP
Ascending:  EE | EA | EA | AA | AD | AD | DG | DG | GG | GB | GB | BB | Be
Descending: eB | BB | BG | BG | GG | GD | GD | DA | DA | AA | AE | AE | EE
Some thirds stay on one string; others cross one string boundary. Keep every landing equal
in volume and preserve the two-note pulse through the D-G and G-B transitions.

CELL DESIGN
- 13 ascending pairs / 26 notes
- repeat the high root at the turnaround
- 13 descending pairs / 26 notes
- 52 notes total per key
- shift the entire hand-and-thumb frame one fret only after the final b3-1 pair

PICKING
Downstroke start: D-U | D-U | D-U | ... through all 26 pairs.
Upstroke start:   U-D | U-D | U-D | ... through all 26 pairs.
Because each pair and the complete 52-note cell are even, every pair and every new key begins
with the same pick orientation. Do not reset at a string crossing, the middle octave, the
repeated high root, or a new key.

RHYTHM
Learning pulse: one pair per beat as eighth notes.
Performance pulse: two pairs per beat as straight sixteenth notes.
Accent the first note of each pair while learning, then remove the accents and equalize all notes.

PRACTICE PROGRESSION
1. A natural minor, first octave only: pairs 1-b3 through b6-8, then reverse.
2. A natural minor, all 13 ascending pairs.
3. A natural minor, all 13 descending pairs.
4. Link the 52-note cell; articulate both high-root notes without resetting.
5. Use two or three neighboring keys with a one-beat pause only after each complete cell.
6. Remove the pause and complete the entire route with both pick starts.

CHROMATIC ROUTE
Up:   A -> Bb -> B -> C -> C# -> D -> Eb -> E -> F -> F# -> G -> G# -> A
Down: G# -> G -> F# -> F -> E -> Eb -> D -> C# -> C -> B -> Bb -> A
Do not replay the high A-natural-minor rung during the down pass unless beginning a new round.

FULL ROUND-TRIP CHALLENGE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Start at A natural minor on low-E fret 5. Complete rungs 01-13 without restarting alternate
picking. Reverse after the fret-17 A-natural-minor cell and return through rungs 12-01. Say
each key name aloud before its first low root. Repeat the entire route with the opposite start.

Route count: 25 cells x 52 notes = 1,300 picked notes.
Tempo rule: add 2 BPM after three clean trips; reduce 4-6 BPM after two errors or signs of tension.

CLEAN-ROUND STANDARD
- no missing, doubled, reversed, or reordered pair
- no pause or accent during the one-degree rollback
- correct G-string backreach, B-string offset, string changes, and assigned fingering
- both high-root notes at the turn are articulated and equal in volume
- no scrape or tempo change during a one-fret shift
- minor and major third qualities remain audible inside one connected line

VARIATIONS AFTER THE BASIC LADDER IS CLEAN
1. Accent the first note of every pair, then remove all pair accents.
2. Accent only the upper note of every pair without rushing the rollback.
3. Say m3 or M3 before every pair in one selected key.
4. Play one pair per beat, then two pairs per beat at the same metronome marking.
5. Begin selected sessions at fret 17 and descend first.
6. Choose a random root fret from 5-17, name the key, and continue either direction.

MASTERY GATE BEFORE LEVEL 28
Complete the full route with a steady two-note pulse, correct interval qualities, clean upper-frame
geometry, silent one-fret shifts, and both pick starts on two separate days.
