GUITAR EXERCISES - SECTION VI, LEVEL 54
CAGED AS A PRACTICAL CHORD, TRIAD, ROOT, ARPEGGIO, AND NEARBY-SCALE MAP

PURPOSE
Use CAGED as an indexing system rather than a five-grip memorization doctrine. Each form identifies root locations, a useful chord fragment, embedded triads, a chord-tone arpeggio, and nearby scale tones. Full C and G forms are optional; partial voicings and triads are the primary performance material.

COMPLETE SOUNDED WORKOUT: 558 attacks plus 45 silent formation prompts.

C-MAJOR REFERENCE CYCLE
C form open -> A form fret 3 -> G form fret 5 -> E form fret 8 -> D form fret 10 -> next C form fret 12.

ROOT-STRING FORMULAS
C form: A-string root r; x r r-1 r-3 r-2 r-3. Additional root on B string r-2.
A form: A-string root r; x r r+2 r+2 r+2 r. Additional root on G string r+2.
G form: low-E root r; r r-1 r-3 r-3 r-3 r. Additional roots on G string r-3 and high e r.
E form: low-E root r; r r+2 r+2 r+1 r r. Additional roots on D string r+2 and high e r.
D form: D-string root r; x x r r+2 r+3 r+2. Additional root on B string r+3.

========================================================================================================================
MODULE A - ROOT MAP AND SILENT FORMATION
========================================================================================================================
For each natural root C-D-E-F-G-A-B, form the C, A, G, E, and D regions silently. Use an octave that keeps all required frets on the fingerboard. Name every root in the form, release, then verify one practical partial and one triad.
Silent count: 5 master forms + 5 practical partials + 35 root/form prompts = 45.

========================================================================================================================
PRACTICE PROGRESSION AND MASTERY
========================================================================================================================
1. Memorize root-string formulas.
2. Build the five C-major forms.
3. Reduce every form to its practical fragment.
4. Arpeggiate all fifteen triads.
5. Convert every region from C major to C minor.
6. Extract all five chord arpeggios.
7. Connect nearby scale tones.
8. Move C through the complete CAGED cycle.
9. Play five C-F-G-C progression cells.
10. Complete random-root prompts and the twenty-bar capstone.

BOUNDARIES
SHAPE WITHOUT ROOT: name every root before sounding the form.
FORCED FULL GRIP: reduce to the practical partial or triad.
LOST INVERSION: name the lowest degree of the triad.
SCALE DETACHED: begin and end on a chord tone.
CYCLE PAUSE: isolate the failing adjacent forms.
FRET SEARCH: calculate from the root-string formula.

MASTERY GATE BEFORE LEVEL 55
- Find all five CAGED regions for any natural root and name the root strings.
- Form every C-major reference, practical partial, and embedded triad without leakage.
- Convert the primary triad in every region from major to minor by moving one voice.
- Play all chord arpeggios, scale routes, the CAGED cycle, and five C-F-G-C cells.
- Complete at least 30 of 35 random root/form prompts on the first attempt.
- Perform the twenty-bar register capstone on two separate days.
