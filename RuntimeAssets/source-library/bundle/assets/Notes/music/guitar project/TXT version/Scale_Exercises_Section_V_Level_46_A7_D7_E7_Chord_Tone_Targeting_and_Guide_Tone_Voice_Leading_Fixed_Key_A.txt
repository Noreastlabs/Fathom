SCALE EXERCISES - SECTION V, LEVEL 46
A7-D7-E7 CHORD-TONE TARGETING AND GUIDE-TONE VOICE LEADING - FIXED KEY A

PURPOSE
Make the twelve-bar A blues harmony audible through intentional chord-tone landings. Keep the A-blues vocabulary
available for motion, but treat the root, third, fifth, and flat seventh of the active dominant chord as structural
targets. Track the third and flat seventh as guide tones through the A7-D7-E7 form.

DOMINANT CHORD TONES
A7 = A C# E G      roles R 3 5 b7
D7 = D F# A C      roles R 3 5 b7
E7 = E G# B D      roles R 3 5 b7

GUIDE-TONE SHELLS
A7: C# + G    D7: F# + C    E7: G# + D
A7 -> D7: C#->C and G->F#.
D7 -> A7: C->C# and F#->G.
A7 -> E7: C#->D and G->G#.
E7 -> A7: D->C# and G#->G.

ERROR DIAGNOSIS
SCALE-RUN AUTOPILOT: remove the fill; sustain one target per bar.
WRONG THIRD: play the fretted target before any bend or slide.
FORM LOSS: return to roots and speak bars 5, 9, 10, 11, and 12.
GUIDE-TONE JUMP: choose the nearer octave; move by half step where possible.
OVERFILLED BAR: leave beat 4 empty and let the target ring.
BEND DRIFT: compare to the fretted target and reduce the bend width.
FRET SEARCH: name string and fret before leaving the current target.
