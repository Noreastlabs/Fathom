GUITAR EXERCISES - SECTION VI, LEVEL 58
SIXTEENTH-NOTE RHYTHM GUITAR, SYNCOPATION, DEAD STROKES, RELEASES, ANTICIPATIONS, FUNK COMPING, AND FULL-SONG CAPSTONE

PURPOSE
Turn the chord, triad, CAGED, progression, and seventh-chord vocabulary from Levels 49-57 into complete rhythm-guitar performance. Maintain a continuous 1-e-&-a right-hand path while deciding whether each stroke produces pitch, a dead contact, a tie, a pressure release, or no contact.

SYMBOLS
P = pitched chord attack
x = dead / muted strum
- = air stroke; no string contact
~ = tie or sustain; no new attack
R = left-hand pressure release
> = accent the following contact
