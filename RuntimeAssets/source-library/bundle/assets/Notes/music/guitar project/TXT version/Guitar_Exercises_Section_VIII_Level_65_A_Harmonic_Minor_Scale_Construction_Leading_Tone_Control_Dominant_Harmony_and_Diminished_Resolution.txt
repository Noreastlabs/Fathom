GUITAR EXERCISES - SECTION VIII, LEVEL 65
A HARMONIC MINOR SCALE CONSTRUCTION, LEADING-TONE CONTROL, DOMINANT HARMONY, AND DIMINISHED RESOLUTION

PURPOSE
Raise G to G# inside A natural minor and train the resulting leading tone, augmented second, strong E/E7 dominant, G# diminished leading-tone chords, and harmonic-minor arpeggio vocabulary.

Degrees: 1 2 b3 4 5 b6 b7 1

A HARMONIC MINOR
