GUITAR EXERCISES - SECTION VI, LEVEL 53
MAJOR AND MINOR TRIADS ACROSS FOUR ADJACENT THREE-STRING SETS

PURPOSE
Transfer the major and minor triad spelling and inversion logic from G-B-e to D-G-B, A-D-G, and E-A-D. Name the bass degree, mute unused strings, connect nearest inversions, use triad fragments melodically, and select register deliberately.

STRING SETS
Strings 1-2-3: G-B-e
Strings 2-3-4: D-G-B
Strings 3-4-5: A-D-G
Strings 4-5-6: E-A-D

TRIAD ORDERS
Major root 1-3-5; first inversion 3-5-1; second inversion 5-1-3.
Minor root 1-b3-5; first inversion b3-5-1; second inversion 5-1-b3.

COMPLETE SOUNDED WORKOUT: 1248 attacks plus 168 silent formations.
