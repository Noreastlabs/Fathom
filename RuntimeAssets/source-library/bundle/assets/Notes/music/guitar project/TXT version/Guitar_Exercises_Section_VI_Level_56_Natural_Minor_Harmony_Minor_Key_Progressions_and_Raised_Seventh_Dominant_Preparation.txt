GUITAR EXERCISES - SECTION VI, LEVEL 56
NATURAL-MINOR HARMONY, MINOR-KEY PROGRESSIONS, AND RAISED-SEVENTH DOMINANT PREPARATION

PURPOSE
Harmonize the natural-minor scale, assign the correct triad quality and function to every degree, compare the natural minor v with the raised-seventh major V and V7, analyze common minor-key progressions, and transpose those functions through A, E, B, F#, C#, and D minor.

NATURAL-MINOR FORMULA
W-H-W-W-H-W-W

HARMONIZED QUALITY SEQUENCE
i minor | ii° diminished | III major | iv minor | v minor | VI major | VII major

FUNCTION FAMILIES
Tonic: i, III, VI
Predominant: ii°, iv, VI
Weak dominant: v, VII
Strong dominant after raising b7 to 7: V, V7, vii°

WRITTEN WORKLOAD
Module A: 42 silent spelling prompts.
Module B: 168 sounded attacks across six harmonized triad ladders.
Modules C-H: 576 attacks across six progression families in six keys.
Module I: 96 nearest-inversion i-iv-V-i attacks.
Module J: 24 written analysis and transposition prompts.
Module K: 96 attacks in the 24-bar minor-key capstone.
COMPLETE SOUNDED WORKOUT: 936 attacks.


========================================================================================================================
MODULE A - NATURAL-MINOR SPELLING, FUNCTION, AND RAISED-SEVENTH PREPARATION
========================================================================================================================
Practice keys in circle order: D -> A -> E -> B -> F# -> C#.
Natural-minor Roman numerals: i ii° III iv v VI VII.
The natural v uses b7. The strong V and V7 use raised 7.
Silent command: name the natural scale, seven natural triads, natural v, raised V or V7, and leading-tone vii° in every key.
