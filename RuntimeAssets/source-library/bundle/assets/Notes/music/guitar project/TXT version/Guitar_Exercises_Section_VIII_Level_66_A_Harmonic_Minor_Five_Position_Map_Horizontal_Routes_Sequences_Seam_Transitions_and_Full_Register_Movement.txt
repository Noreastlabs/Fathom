ERROR DIAGNOSIS
LOST FRAME: name the low-E start degree and fret span.
EARLY SHIFT: stay through the final shared note; move after it.
AUG2 COLLAPSE: play F, sing G#, relocate, then land.
WEAK LEADING TONE: hold G#, then resolve to A without rushing.
PICK RESET: air-pick the 18-, 36-, 42-, or 90-tone count.
RINGING STRING: reduce pressure and mute before the destination.
FORM LOSS: return to one chord-tone target per bar.
