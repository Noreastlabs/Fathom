SCALE EXERCISES - SECTION IV, LEVEL 34
A MAJOR PENTATONIC FIVE-POSITION CONNECTION - FIXED-KEY NECK MAP

PURPOSE
Stop transposing one shape. Keep the key fixed as A major pentatonic and move through five common
positions from the low-E root frame at frets 4-7 through the octave frame at frets 14-17. Learn each
vertical position, connect adjacent positions around one shared anchor note on every string, then link
all five frames in a forward and reverse zigzag.

IMPORTANT POSITION-LABEL NOTE
Pentatonic box numbers vary among methods. This workbook numbers positions in ascending-neck order,
beginning with the A-root frame around low-E fret 5. Use the fret spans, degree maps, and root locations
as the authoritative identity of each position.

A MAJOR-PENTATONIC FORMULA
Notes: A B C# E F#
Degrees: 1 2 3 5 6
Intervals: whole - whole - minor third - whole - minor third

POSITION SUMMARY

    Ascending degrees: 1 2 3 5 6 1 2 3 5 6 1 2

    Ascending degrees: 2 3 5 6 1 2 3 5 6 1 2 3

    Ascending degrees: 3 5 6 1 2 3 5 6 1 2 3 5

    Ascending degrees: 5 6 1 2 3 5 6 1 2 3 5 6

    Ascending degrees: 6 1 2 3 5 6 1 2 3 5 6 1

PICKING AND COUNTS
Each vertical position cell contains 24 notes: 12 ascending and 12 descending with the high endpoint repeated.
Each shared-anchor seam cell contains 30 notes: six five-note string groups. Every five-note group flips the
starting stroke on the next string, but the complete 30-note cell is even.
The forward zigzag contains 60 notes; the reverse zigzag contains 60 notes. Repeat high-e fret 17 at the
turn when linking them, producing an even 120-note circuit.
Five position cells = 120 notes. Four seam cells = 120 notes. Linked zigzag = 120 notes.
Complete Level 34 workout = 360 notes.

STRICT ALTERNATE PICKING
Complete separate D-start and U-start passes. Do not restart at a string crossing, shared anchor, position
boundary, root, outer-string turn, or the forward/reverse turnaround.

========================================================================================================================
PRACTICE PROGRESSION
========================================================================================================================
1. Map P1-P5 and say each starting degree.
2. Play every 24-note vertical cell with both pick starts.
3. Learn all four 30-note shared-anchor seam cells.
4. Play the 60-note forward zigzag without a seam pause.
5. Play the 60-note reverse zigzag without a seam pause.
6. Link forward and reverse as one 120-note circuit.
7. Accent every A root, then remove the accents while continuing to hear A as tonic.

ERROR DIAGNOSIS
LOST KEY CENTER: Stop on the nearest A root, sing A, and restart the local frame.
WRONG BOX: Name the position span and its first low-E degree before playing.
EARLY SHIFT: Remain in the lower frame through the shared anchor; move only after it sounds.
NOISY SEAM: Release pressure, move the thumb with the hand, and reduce the tempo by 4-6 BPM.
PICK RESET: Mute the strings and air-pick the exact 12-, 30-, or 60-note count.
TENSION: Stop, shake out, and practice one seam instead of forcing the full route.

ADVANCEMENT RULES
Add 2 BPM after three clean circuits at the current route length.
After two errors, reduce 4-6 BPM or shorten the route to one adjacent-position seam.
Increase route length before speed when the shift is weak. Increase speed before route length when the map is secure.

MASTERY GATE BEFORE LEVEL 35
Name all five positions and fret spans; play each vertical cell with D-start and U-start; shift at every
shared anchor without a scrape or hesitation; complete the linked 120-note zigzag on two separate days;
identify every A root by sight and sound; and recover the correct position after a deliberate random stop.
