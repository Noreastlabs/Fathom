GUITAR EXERCISES - SECTION VI, LEVEL 52
MAJOR AND MINOR TRIADS - ROOT POSITION, FIRST INVERSION, SECOND INVERSION - TOP THREE STRINGS

PURPOSE
Compress major and minor harmony into three-note voicings on strings G, B, and high e. Spell each triad, identify root/third/fifth, distinguish three inversions by the bass voice, switch quality by moving the third, connect nearest inversions through progressions, and apply blocked and arpeggiated patterns.

TRIAD FORMULAS
Major: 1 3 5
Minor: 1 b3 5
Root position: 1-3-5 or 1-b3-5
First inversion: 3-5-1 or b3-5-1
Second inversion: 5-1-3 or 5-1-b3

COMPLETE SOUNDED WORKLOAD: 976 attacks plus 42 silent triad formations.
