MASTERY STANDARD
On two separate days, reconstruct an unfamiliar 16-32 bar excerpt without a supplied chart. Score 0-2 for form, bass, harmony, rhythm, melodic targets, and arrangement. Pass at 10/12 or better, with no zero in form, bass, harmony, or rhythm, then perform the reconstructed arrangement without stopping.
