SCALE EXERCISES - SECTION IV, LEVEL 33
MAJOR PENTATONIC INTERVAL PAIRS AND MELODIC SKIPS - TWO-OCTAVE SIX-STRING NECK LADDER: A TO A

PURPOSE
Keep the Level 31 two-octave major-pentatonic frame and reorganize its eleven ordered scale positions into
two interval-focused modules. Module A skips one pentatonic tone to create adjacent-string interval pairs.
Module B makes a wider leap, then resolves to the skipped upper tone. Practice each module separately, link
both modules, then move the complete hand-and-thumb frame exactly one fret. Begin with A major pentatonic
at low-E fret 5, climb to the octave A at fret 17, then reverse and return one fret at a time.

MAJOR-PENTATONIC FORMULA
Degrees per octave: 1 2 3 5 6
Intervals: whole - whole - minor third - whole - minor third
Two-octave ordered positions: 1 2 3 5 6 8 9 10 12 13 15.

TWO-OCTAVE SIX-STRING SHAPE - ROOT FRET R ON LOW E
Low E string:  R, R+2               Fingers 2,4                  Positions 1,2
A string:      R-1, R+2             Fingers 1,4                  Positions 3,5
D string:      R-1, R+2             Fingers 1,4                  Positions 6,8
G string:      R-1, R+1             Fingers 1,3                  Positions 9,10
B string:      R, R+2               Fingers 2,4                  Positions 12,13
High e string: R                    Finger 2                     Position 15
The four-fret frame runs from R-1 through R+2. Do not change the Level 31 fingering.

MODULE A - SKIP-ONE INTERVAL PAIRS
UP:   1-3 | 2-5 | 3-6 | 5-8 | 6-9 | 8-10 | 9-12 | 10-13 | 12-15
INT:  M3  | P4  | P4  | P4  | P4  | M3   | P4   | P4    | P4
DOWN: 15-12 | 13-10 | 12-9 | 10-8 | 9-6 | 8-5 | 6-3 | 5-2 | 3-1
Each pair skips one pentatonic position and lands on the next physical string. The module contains 18 pairs
and 36 notes. Native pulse: one two-note pair per beat as eighth notes.
String paths up:   EA | EA | AD | AD | DG | DG | GB | GB | Be
String paths down: eB | BG | BG | GD | GD | DA | DA | AE | AE

MODULE B - WIDE-LEAP RESOLVE
UP:   1-5-3 | 2-6-5 | 3-8-6 | 5-9-8 | 6-10-9 | 8-12-10 | 9-13-12 | 10-15-13
DOWN: 15-10-12 | 13-9-10 | 12-8-9 | 10-6-8 | 9-5-6 | 8-3-5 | 6-2-3 | 5-1-2
The first two notes leap across three ordered pentatonic positions; the third note resolves to the skipped
upper position. First-leap qualities are P5 or m6. Resolution qualities are M2 or m3. The module contains
16 triplets and 48 notes. Native pulse: one eighth-note triplet per beat.
String paths up:   EAA | EDA | ADD | AGD | DGG | DBG | GBB | GeB
String paths down: eGB | BGG | BDG | GDD | GAD | DAA | DEA | AEE

PICKING AND CELL RULE
Module A uses even two-note groups, so every pair begins with the original pick orientation.
Module B uses odd three-note groups, so the group-starting stroke alternates at every boundary.
Module A contains 36 notes and Module B contains 48 notes; both totals are even. Therefore Module B and
every new key begin with the original starting stroke. Do not reset at a pair, triplet, string crossing,
skipped string, middle root, high-root turn, module boundary, or one-fret shift.
A D-start: D-U | D-U | D-U ...
B D-start: D-U-D | U-D-U | D-U-D ...
Use the mirrored patterns for an upstroke start.

RHYTHM MODES
Grouped mode: Module A = one eighth-note pair per beat; Module B = one eighth-note triplet per beat.
Displacement mode: after the grouped pulse is secure, play continuous sixteenth notes and let the accents
move through 4/4 without changing the degree order.

NECK ROUTE
UP:   A(5) > Bb(6) > B(7) > C(8) > Db(9) > D(10) > Eb(11) > E(12) > F(13) > Gb(14) > G(15) > Ab(16) > A(17)
DOWN: A(17) > Ab(16) > G(15) > Gb(14) > F(13) > E(12) > Eb(11) > D(10) > Db(9) > C(8) > B(7) > Bb(6) > A(5)

COUNTS
Module A: 36 notes / 18 beats per key; 25-cell round trip = 900 notes.
Module B: 48 notes / 16 beats per key; 25-cell round trip = 1,200 notes.
Linked A+B: 84 notes / 34 beats per key; 25-cell round trip = 2,100 notes / 850 beats.
