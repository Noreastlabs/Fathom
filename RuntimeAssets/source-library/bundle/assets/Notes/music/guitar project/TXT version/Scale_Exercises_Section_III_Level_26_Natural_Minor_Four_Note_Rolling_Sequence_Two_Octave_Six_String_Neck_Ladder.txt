SCALE EXERCISES - SECTION III, LEVEL 26
NATURAL MINOR FOUR-NOTE ROLLING SEQUENCE - TWO-OCTAVE SIX-STRING NECK LADDER: A TO A

PURPOSE
Extend the Level 25 four-note rolling sequence across the complete two-octave, six-string
natural-minor frame. Complete the 96-note up/down cell, release pressure, move the entire
hand-and-thumb frame exactly one fret, and repeat through every natural-minor key. Begin with
A natural minor at low-E fret 5, climb to A natural minor at fret 17, then reverse and return
one fret at a time.

WHAT CHANGES FROM LEVEL 25
Level 25 taught the four-note overlap inside the compact one-octave frame. Level 26 preserves
the same overlap and extends it through positions 1-15 across all six strings. The new work is
maintaining the sequence through the middle octave, G-string backreach, B-string tuning offset,
single high-e endpoint, repeated high root, and longer uninterrupted alternate-picking cycle.

IMPORTANT INTERPRETATION
Moving one fixed scale shape by one fret transposes it. A natural minor is the starting rung,
not the key of every rung. A fixed-key A-natural-minor exercise in every neck position requires
changing the note layout and remains a separate branch.

NATURAL-MINOR FORMULA
One octave: 1 2 b3 4 5 b6 b7 8
Two octaves: 1 2 b3 4 5 b6 b7 8 9 b10 11 12 b13 b14 15
Intervals: whole - half - whole - whole - half - whole - whole, then repeat
The tablature uses a Pos row for positions 1-15; this degree map identifies the altered tones.

TWO-OCTAVE SIX-STRING SHAPE - ROOT FRET R ON LOW E
Low E string: R, R+2, R+3          Fingers 1,3,4        Positions 1,2,3
A string:     R, R+2, R+3          Fingers 1,3,4        Positions 4,5,6
D string:     R, R+2               Fingers 1,3          Positions 7,8
G string:     R-1, R, R+2          Fingers 1,2,4        Positions 9,10,11
B string:     R, R+1, R+3          Fingers 1,2,4        Positions 12,13,14
High e:       R                    Finger 1             Position 15
The complete map spans R-1 through R+3. Do not hold a static five-fret stretch. Release and
rebuild the frame for the G-string backreach and again for the B-string offset.

ROLLING FOUR-NOTE SEQUENCE
ASCENDING:
1-2-b3-4 | 2-b3-4-5 | b3-4-5-b6 | 4-5-b6-b7 | 5-b6-b7-8 | b6-b7-8-9 |
b7-8-9-b10 | 8-9-b10-11 | 9-b10-11-12 | b10-11-12-b13 | 11-12-b13-b14 | 12-b13-b14-15
DESCENDING:
15-b14-b13-12 | b14-b13-12-11 | b13-12-11-b10 | 12-11-b10-9 | 11-b10-9-8 |
b10-9-8-b7 | 9-8-b7-b6 | 8-b7-b6-5 | b7-b6-5-4 | b6-5-4-b3 | 5-4-b3-2 | 4-b3-2-1
Each new group begins one scale position farther along. Three notes overlap from one group to
the next. At each boundary, the line drops a diatonic third and resumes forward motion.
Do not pause, accent, or reset at a group boundary.

STRING-CROSSING MAP
UP:   EEEA | EEAA | EAAA | AAAD | AADD | ADDG | DDGG | DGGG | GGGB | GGBB | GBBB | BBBe
DOWN: eBBB | BBBG | BBGG | BGGG | GGGD | GGDD | GDDA | DDAA | DAAA | AAAE | AAEE | AEEE
The route includes no-crossing, early-crossing, midpoint-crossing, and late-crossing groups.
Keep every landing inside the same sixteenth-note pulse, especially at the D-G and G-B changes.

CELL AND PICKING RULE
The complete cell contains 12 ascending groups and 12 descending groups: 24 groups and 96 notes.
The final high root of the ascent is immediately followed by the same high root to begin the
descent. Because each group has four notes, every group begins with the same pick stroke. Because
the full 96-note cell is even, each new key also begins with the original pick orientation.
D-start groups: D-U-D-U | D-U-D-U | D-U-D-U ... through all 24 groups.
U-start groups: U-D-U-D | U-D-U-D | U-D-U-D ... through all 24 groups.
Do not restart at a group, string crossing, middle octave, repeated high root, or one-fret shift.

NECK ROUTE
UP:   A(5) > Bb(6) > B(7) > C(8) > C#(9) > D(10) > Eb(11) > E(12) > F(13) > F#(14) > G(15) > G#(16) > A(17)
DOWN: A(17) > G#(16) > G(15) > F#(14) > F(13) > E(12) > Eb(11) > D(10) > C#(9) > C(8) > B(7) > Bb(6) > A(5)

PRACTICE RULE
Use sixteenth notes: one four-note group per beat. Begin 15-20 BPM below your clean Level 25
tempo. Master the 12 ascending A-natural-minor groups and 12 descending groups separately, then
link them through the repeated high-root turn. Use 2-3 neighboring rungs and pause one beat after
each 96-note cell. During the pause, release pressure and move the complete frame one fret. Repeat
with both pick starts. Remove the pause, connect the ladder, reverse after the fret-17 cell, and
return to fret 5. Add 2 BPM after three clean round trips. Reduce 4-6 BPM after two errors, an
uneven group pulse, missed upper-frame geometry, dragged shift noise, a crossing accent, or rising
tension. Advance after the full route is clean with both pick starts on two separate days.

1. Accent note 1 of every group, then remove all accents.
2. Repeat later passes with the accent on notes 2, 3, and 4.
3. Accent only positions 1, 3, 5, 7, 8, 10, 12, 14, and 15.
4. Play two groups per beat at half the metronome marking.
5. Begin at fret 17 and descend first.
6. Enter at a random root fret 5-17, name the key, and continue in either direction.
These are Level 26 practice variations, not separate full levels.

MASTERY GATE BEFORE LEVEL 27
Complete the full route with continuous sixteenth-note pulse, correct six-string natural-minor
geometry, silent one-fret shifts, and both pick starts on two separate days.
