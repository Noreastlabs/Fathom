SCALE EXERCISES - SECTION IV, LEVEL 37
A MAJOR PENTATONIC I-IV-V CHORD-TONE TARGETING AND VOICE LEADING
FIXED KEY A - FIVE POSITIONS - A, D, AND E MAJOR HARMONY

PURPOSE
Turn the fixed-key A-major-pentatonic map into harmony-aware phrasing. Keep the same five notes and five positions,
but stop treating every note as equally stable. Hear the active I, IV, or V chord; land on a strong chord tone at
the change; use the remaining pentatonic notes as motion; then resolve before the harmony changes again.

A MAJOR PENTATONIC
Notes: A B C# E F#
Degrees: 1 2 3 5 6
I-IV-V chords: A major = A C# E; D major = D F# A; E major = E G# B.

CORE TARGETS AVAILABLE INSIDE A MAJOR PENTATONIC
I - A major: A root, C# third, E fifth.
IV - D major: F# third, A fifth. The D root is not in A major pentatonic.
V - E major: E root, B fifth. The G# third is not in A major pentatonic.
Module D adds D and G# temporarily as chord-completion tones; they are not new pentatonic degrees.

CHORD-ROLE MATRIX
Note | Over A | Over D | Over E
A    | R      | 5      | 11    
B    | 9      | 13     | 5     
C#   | 3      | M7     | 13    
E    | 5      | 9      | R     
F#   | 13     | 3      | 9     
A over E functions as 11/sus4 tension. C# over D functions as major-7 tension. Use either as motion, not an
unresolved downbeat landing, until the ear clearly hears the intended color.

PRACTICE PROGRESSION
1. Speak the I-IV-V chord tones and the five-note role matrix.
2. Complete one Position-1 target cell for each chord with both pick starts.
3. Play all fifteen target cells; hear the active chord before each cell.
4. Hold the three voice-leading routes as whole notes in all five positions.
5. Play one four-bar phrase and verify every beat-1 landing.
6. Add all five phrases, then one Level-36 articulation per bar.
7. Add D* and G#* through the five completion cadences.
8. Complete the 12-bar model chorus, then create a new fill while preserving its targets.

ERROR DIAGNOSIS
SCALE-RUN AUTOPILOT: stop the fill; play only one whole-note target per chord.
WEAK CHORD CHANGE: sing the next target before the bar arrives; reduce the fill to two notes.
D-CHORD C# LANDING: treat C# as major-7 tension and resolve immediately to D*, B, or a printed strong target.
E-CHORD A LANDING: treat A as suspended 11 tension and resolve to G#*, B, or E.
FRET SEARCH: name the string and fret before leaving the current target.
OVERFILLED BAR: leave beat 4 empty; silence is part of the phrase.
PICK OR ARTICULATION RESET: preserve the target rhythm first; expression is secondary.
