SCALE EXERCISES - SECTION V, LEVEL 48
A BLUES CALL-AND-RESPONSE AND IMPROVISATION SYNTHESIS - FIXED KEY A
TWELVE-BAR FORM - FIVE POSITIONS - THREE-CHORUS CAPSTONE

PURPOSE
Combine the A-minor-pentatonic map, the flat-five blue note, shuffle rhythm, A7-D7-E7 chord targets,
major/minor color control, articulation, position movement, motif development, and deliberate space into
complete musical conversations. The objective is not to play more notes. The objective is to make the form,
the active chord, the motif, the phrase boundary, and the dynamic direction audible without losing the pulse.

PITCH POOLS
A minor pentatonic: A C D E G          degrees 1 b3 4 5 b7
A blues scale:        A C D Eb E G       adds b5
A major pentatonic:   A B C# E F#       degrees 1 2 3 5 6
Completion tone:      G# may appear as the third of E7. F may appear only as a marked chromatic approach.

TWELVE-BAR FORM
| A7 | A7 | A7 | A7 |
| D7 | D7 | A7 | A7 |
| E7 | D7 | A7 | E7 |

PHRASE GRAMMAR
A call states a rhythm, contour, register, color, or articulation and leaves a question. A response preserves
at least one recognizable feature while changing the chord target, contour, register, density, or ending.
Silence is counted. A position shift must sound like part of the sentence. The strongest target should arrive
because the harmony changed, not because the phrase ran out of notes.

TAB LEGEND
P = picked attack; h = hammer-on; p = pull-off; s = slide arrival; b = bend; b< = blue-third shade;
r = rest; = = held tone. Color row: C common anchor, m minor-only, b5 blue note, M major-only, * completion.

MODULE K - THREE-CHORUS CONTROLLED IMPROVISATION CAPSTONE
========================================================================================================================
CHORUS 1 - MINOR-CENTERED SPACE
Use A minor pentatonic only. Maximum four sounding tones per bar. Use no more than two positions. Leave at
least one full beat of silence in every two-bar pair. Make bars 5 and 9 audible through D or E targets.

CHORUS 2 - BLUES VOCABULARY AND MOTIF DEVELOPMENT
Add Eb, slides, legato, bends, releases, and vibrato. Choose one seed motif and transform it in every two-bar
pair. Move through at least four positions. Use no more than one conspicuous articulation per bar.

CHORUS 3 - CHORD-AWARE COLOR SYNTHESIS
Mix major and minor pentatonic color. Target C# over A7, F# or C over D7, and G#/B/D/E over E7. Use all
five positions, include a dynamic climax, and choose either a tonic close or a functional E7 turnaround.

RESPONSE CARDS
Echo: preserve rhythm. Reverse: invert contour. Reposition: answer in the next box. Delay: enter one eighth later.
Reduce: use fewer notes. Expand: add one articulation. Recolor: major versus minor. Target: change only the landing.

FORM RECOVERY LANDMARKS
Bar 1 A7: beginning. Bar 5 D7: first change. Bar 7 A7: return. Bar 9 E7: top of the form. Bar 11 A7:
tonic return. Bar 12 E7 or A7: turnaround or close. If lost, stop playing, keep counting, and re-enter at the
next landmark instead of inventing an extra measure.

Rhythmic space: 0 absent / 1 inconsistent / 2 controlled.
Chord targeting: 0 absent / 1 inconsistent / 2 controlled.
Bend and slide intonation: 0 absent / 1 inconsistent / 2 controlled.

Position movement: 0 absent / 1 inconsistent / 2 controlled.

4. Transform the seed motif in seven ways without changing tempo.
