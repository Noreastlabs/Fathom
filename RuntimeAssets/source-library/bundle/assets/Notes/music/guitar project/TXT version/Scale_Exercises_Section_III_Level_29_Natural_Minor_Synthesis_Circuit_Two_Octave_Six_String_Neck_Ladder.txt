SCALE EXERCISES - SECTION III, LEVEL 29
NATURAL MINOR SYNTHESIS CIRCUIT - TWO-OCTAVE SIX-STRING NECK LADDER
================================================================================================================================================================

PURPOSE
Level 29 combines the established two-octave natural-minor movements from Levels 22, 24, 26, 27, and 28 into
one uninterrupted circuit. Complete all five modules in the same key before shifting the entire hand-and-thumb
frame one fret. Re-articulate the low root at every module boundary while preserving the alternate-picking cycle.

FIVE-MODULE CELL
A. Linear scale:          30 notes - low root to high root, repeat high root, return to low root
B. Three-note roll:       78 notes - 13 ascending triplets and 13 descending triplets
C. Four-note roll:        96 notes - 12 ascending groups and 12 descending groups
D. Diatonic thirds:       52 notes - 13 ascending pairs and 13 descending pairs
E. Diatonic triads:       66 notes - 11 ascending triads and 11 descending triads
TOTAL:                   322 notes per key

MODULE ORDER
Linear -> Three-note roll -> Four-note roll -> Diatonic thirds -> Diatonic triads -> shift one fret

NATURAL-MINOR FORMULA
One octave: 1 2 b3 4 5 b6 b7 8
Two octaves: 1 2 b3 4 5 b6 b7 8 9 b10 11 12 b13 b14 15
Intervals: whole - half - whole - whole - half - whole - whole, then repeat

PICKING
All five module counts are even. With a downstroke start, every module and every new key begins on a downstroke;
with an upstroke start, each begins on an upstroke. Do not reset at group boundaries, string changes, octave turns,
module boundaries, the G-string backreach, the B-string offset, or one-fret shifts.

RHYTHM
Learning mode: one note per metronome click through the complete circuit.
Native-pulse mode:
- Linear scale: 30 eighth notes = 15 beats
- Three-note roll: 78 eighth-note triplets = 26 beats
- Four-note roll: 96 sixteenth notes = 24 beats
- Diatonic thirds: 52 eighth notes = 26 beats
- Diatonic triads: 66 eighth-note triplets = 22 beats
TOTAL: 113 quarter-note beats per 322-note circuit

PRACTICE PROGRESSION
1. Learn each module separately in A natural minor.
2. Link adjacent module pairs: A-B, B-C, C-D, and D-E.
3. Play the complete A-natural-minor circuit with a short pause at every module boundary.
4. Remove the pauses while retaining a light accent on each new module root.
5. Remove the accents and use two or three neighboring keys.
6. Complete longer route sections with both a downstroke and an upstroke start.

CHROMATIC ROUTE
Up:   A -> Bb -> B -> C -> C# -> D -> Eb -> E -> F -> F# -> G -> G# -> A
Down: G# -> G -> F# -> F -> E -> Eb -> D -> C# -> C -> B -> Bb -> A
Do not replay the high A-natural-minor rung during the down pass unless beginning a new round.

ROUTE COUNT
25 circuits x 322 notes = 8,050 picked notes.
25 circuits x 113 native-pulse beats = 2,825 quarter-note beats.
The full route is a capstone endurance test, not a required daily warm-up.
