GUITAR EXERCISES - SECTION VI, LEVEL 51
MOVABLE BARRE-CHORD FAMILIES, ROOT FINDING, PARTIAL BARRES, AND PRESSURE MANAGEMENT

PURPOSE
Convert the open E and A major/minor families into movable chords. Find roots on low E and A strings, build from partial to full barres, use minimum pressure, switch chord quality, move between shape families, and apply the shapes to progressions.

CORE SHAPES
E-shape major: R 5 R 3 5 R
E-shape minor: R 5 R b3 5 R
A-shape major: x R 5 R 3 5
A-shape minor: x R 5 R b3 5

COMPLETE SOUNDED WORKLOAD: 996 attacks plus 56 silent chord landings.

MODULE B - BARRE PRESSURE AND PARTIAL-BARRE LADDER
========================================================================================================================

2-string barre: B + high e; frets 5 5; 2 attacks.
3-string barre: G + B + high e; frets 5 5 5; 3 attacks.
4-string barre: D + G + B + high e; frets 5 5 5 5; 4 attacks.
5-string barre: A through high e; frets 5 5 5 5 5; 5 attacks.
6-string barre: low E through high e; frets 5 5 5 5 5 5; 6 attacks.
Repeat all five stages at frets 5 and 7. 40 picked-string attacks.
Stop for pain, numbness, tingling, or tension that does not relax after release.

========================================================================================================================
ERROR REPAIR AND MASTERY
========================================================================================================================
DEAD BARRED STRING: move index angle or vertical position before adding pressure.
DEAD CHORD-FINGER STRING: curve the neighboring finger and move closer to the fret.
LOW E LEAK IN A SHAPE: touch low E with the index tip without pressing it.
HIGH E DEAD IN A-MAJOR: roll the ring mini-barre away from high e.
SOUNDING SHIFT: release pressure fully before moving the root fret.
THUMB FATIGUE: reduce clamp, use arm support, and shorten the voicing.
QUALITY ERROR: isolate the G-string or B-string quality note.
MASTERY GATE: find every natural root; form all four families; scan every string cleanly; switch quality at seven roots; transfer between same-fret families; complete all root ladders and progressions; perform both capstones on two separate days; finish without pain, numbness, or lingering thumb tension.
NEXT LEVEL: Level 52 maps major and minor triads in root position, first inversion, and second inversion.
