SCALE EXERCISES - SECTION V, LEVEL 44
A BLUES RESOLUTION VOCABULARY, LICK CONSTRUCTION, AND FUNCTIONAL TURNAROUNDS - FIXED KEY A

PURPOSE
Turn the Level 43 blue-note map into a compact, transformable blues vocabulary. Train three primary
resolution gestures, apply them to one-bar rhythm families and two-bar conversations, translate the
same ideas through all five positions, distinguish tonic endings from turnaround endings, and build
functional bar-11/bar-12 lines that point toward E7 or close on A.

CORE PITCH POOL
A blues scale: A C D Eb E G
Degrees:       1 b3 4 b5 5 b7

MARKED SUPPORT NOTES
C# = 3*       Major-third destination for the blue-third gesture over A or A7.
F# = 6*       Chromatic or chord-aware passing note in selected turnarounds.
F  = b6*      Lower chromatic approach to E in selected turnarounds.
B  = 2*/E7-5 Fifth of E7 when a phrase points toward the V chord.
G# = 7*/E7-3 Third of E7 in the written E7 arpeggio.
An asterisk means the pitch is not being added to the six-note A-blues scale. It has a specific job.

PRIMARY RESOLUTION VOCABULARY
1. BLUE THIRD:     b3 -> 3* or b3 -> 3* -> b3
2. FLAT FIVE:      4-b5-5 and 5-b5-4
3. TONIC CADENCE:  b7 -> 1

MODULE D - FIVE ONE-BAR LICK FAMILIES
==============================================================================================================================
Apply all four rhythm templates to every eight-tone family.
A. STRAIGHT EIGHTHS: 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 &
  X X X X X X X X
  Eight equal attacks or sounding events.
B. SHUFFLE PAIRS: 1-trip-let 2-trip-let 3-trip-let 4-trip-let
  X - X | X - X | X - X | X - X
  Place each pair on the outer triplet subdivisions.
C. SYNCOPATED SIXTEENTHS: 1 e & a 2 e & a 3 e & a 4 e & a
  - X - X | X - X - | - X - X | X - X -
  Preserve the rests; do not fill the empty slots.
D. BURST + SPACE: beat 1 | beat 2 | beat 3 | beat 4
  3-note burst | rest | 3-note burst | 2 notes
  Keep the final pair relaxed after the silent second beat.

==============================================================================================================================
PRACTICE PROGRESSION
==============================================================================================================================
1. Calibrate all six blue-third targets before bending.
2. Complete the six flat-five resolution sites as picked triplets.
3. Complete all six b7-to-1 sites using picked, slide, and legato versions.
4. Learn one eight-tone family and apply all four rhythm templates.
5. Add the remaining four families without increasing tempo.
6. Practice each call alone, each answer alone, then link the two bars.
7. Translate both core licks through all five positions.
8. Complete the four register conversations and five ending pairs.
9. Learn one turnaround at a time over bars 11-12 of a backing track.
10. Play all 16 recombination choices; keep only the combinations that sound intentional.

ERROR DIAGNOSIS
BLUE THIRD SHARP OR FLAT: replay the fretted C# target and reduce the bend width.
b5 SOUNDS RANDOM: decide whether it passes, sustains, or begins a chromatic slide before playing.
ROOT ARRIVES LATE: isolate the final G-to-A motion and remove the preceding notes.
RHYTHM VARIANTS SOUND IDENTICAL: exaggerate the rests and accents, then normalize dynamics.
CALL AND ANSWER UNRELATED: preserve one rhythmic fragment or contour from the call.
POSITION TRANSLATION CHANGES DEGREES: name the notes before copying the phrase.
TURNAROUND CLOSES TOO EARLY: finish on E, B, G#, or a written E7 shape rather than A.
CHROMATIC SOUP: mark every starred support note and state its job aloud.
PAIN OR EXCESS TENSION: stop bends, reduce the range, and return to picked versions.

ADVANCEMENT RULES
Add 2 BPM only after three clean repetitions of the current phrase and rhythm. After two pitch or rhythm
errors, reduce 4-6 BPM or remove one phrase half. Expand the vocabulary only after the listener can hear
whether a phrase closes on A or points toward E7.

MASTERY GATE BEFORE LEVEL 45
Play all six blue-third sites in tune; complete the flat-five and b7-to-1 maps; perform five lick families
in four rhythmic forms; link five call-and-response pairs; translate two core licks through five positions;
execute four register conversations, ten ending choices, four functional turnarounds, and all 16 recombination
choices; then demonstrate one tonic close and one bar-12 turnaround on two separate days.

NEXT LEVEL
Level 45 isolates shuffle feel, triplet placement, rests, pickups, stop-time phrasing, twelve-bar navigation,
and turnaround timing so the vocabulary remains inside the form.
