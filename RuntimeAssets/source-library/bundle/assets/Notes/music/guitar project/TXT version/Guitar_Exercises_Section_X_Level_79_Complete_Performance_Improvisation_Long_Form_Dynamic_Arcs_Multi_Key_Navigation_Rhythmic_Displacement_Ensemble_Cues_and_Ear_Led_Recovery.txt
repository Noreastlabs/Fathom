GUITAR EXERCISES - SECTION X, LEVEL 79
COMPLETE-PERFORMANCE IMPROVISATION: LONG-FORM DYNAMIC ARCS, MULTI-KEY NAVIGATION, RHYTHMIC DISPLACEMENT, ENSEMBLE CUES, AND EAR-LED RECOVERY

PURPOSE
Combine Section X skills inside one continuous 48-bar performance. Preserve pulse, form, key center, role, harmonic targets, rhythmic identity, cues, dynamics, and recovery without restarting.

CONTROL HIERARCHY
1. Pulse / bar count
2. Section / cue
3. Key center / harmony
4. Role: accompaniment or lead
5. Structural target
6. Register / density
7. Articulation / ornament

COMPLETE WRITTEN WORKLOAD: 1,200 events plus the uninterrupted capstone take.

MODULE A - LONG-FORM DYNAMIC ARC
====================================================================================================================
1-4 | Intro | p | 1-2 contacts/bar | low | leave maximum space
5-12 | Verse A | mp | 4 contacts/bar | low-mid | steady comp; no fills until bar 8
13-16 | Pre | mf | 4-6 contacts/bar | middle | rise register each bar
17-24 | Chorus | f | 6-8 contacts/bar | wide | open voicings; short lead answers
25-32 | Verse B | mp | 2-4 contacts/bar | middle | drop density immediately
33-40 | Bridge | mf -> f | 4-7 contacts/bar | middle-high | build to second peak
41-44 | Finale A | f | 6 contacts/bar | high | maintain energy
45-48 | Finale B | mf -> pp | 4 -> 1 | descending | release into final tonic
Practice the arc first with roots only, then shells, then full arrangement. Dynamics must remain audible when note count is reduced.

MODULE D - ENSEMBLE CUES
====================================================================================================================
Head nod | lead time next bar | response change section | if missed: roots/shells until next landmark
Hand lift | lead time 2 beats | response prepare held hit | if missed: roots/shells until next landmark
Eye contact | lead time next phrase | response role handoff | if missed: roots/shells until next landmark
Drum fill | lead time end of fill | response enter next section | if missed: roots/shells until next landmark
Bass walk-up | lead time next bar | response raise register | if missed: roots/shells until next landmark
Held chord | lead time release cue | response stop density; sustain | if missed: roots/shells until next landmark
Two-hit figure | lead time immediate | response match ensemble hits | if missed: roots/shells until next landmark
Count "4" | lead time next bar | response new section | if missed: roots/shells until next landmark
Unexpected extra bar | lead time one bar | response repeat minimal motif | if missed: roots/shells until next landmark
Early chorus cue | lead time next bar | response skip planned fill | if missed: roots/shells until next landmark
Late solo cue | lead time one bar | response continue comping | if missed: roots/shells until next landmark
Cutoff cue | lead time immediate | response mute cleanly | if missed: roots/shells until next landmark

CUE-TO-ROLE CONVERSION
Visual nod: COMP -> LEAD. finish shell; sing next target; enter high register. Keep the current bar count.
Visual nod: LEAD -> COMP. resolve by beat 3; reform chord. Keep the current bar count.
Drum fill: COMP -> HIT. mute groove; match final ensemble hit. Keep the current bar count.
Held chord: LEAD -> SILENCE. resolve to guide tone; stop adding notes. Keep the current bar count.
Bass walk-up: COMP -> HIGH COMP. move to upper triad on next bar. Keep the current bar count.
Count cue: SILENCE -> LEAD. pre-hear beat-one target during count. Keep the current bar count.
Cutoff: ANY -> SILENCE. release pressure and mute. Keep the current bar count.
Extra bar: ANY -> MINIMAL. repeat one root/guide-tone idea only. Keep the current bar count.

DENSITY / REGISTER MATRIX
Sparse comp -> dense lead: outgoing 1 shell/bar; incoming 5 tones/bar; register low -> high
Dense comp -> sparse lead: outgoing 8th stabs; incoming 2 tones/bar; register mid -> mid
Low comp -> high lead: outgoing roots/shells; incoming 4-tone motif; register low -> high
High comp -> low lead: outgoing triads; incoming bass-register arpeggio; register high -> low
Muted comp -> open lead: outgoing dead/short; incoming sustained; register mid
Open comp -> muted lead: outgoing sustain; incoming short targets; register mid
Straight comp -> displaced lead: outgoing quarters; incoming motif offset by 16th; register mid-high
Odd comp -> straight lead: outgoing 3+3+2; incoming quarter-note targets; register mid

MODULE F - EAR-LED RECOVERY
====================================================================================================================
Wrong scale note: immediate repair = nearest chord tone; next action = continue pulse.
Wrong chord quality: immediate repair = root + third shell; next action = restore quality.
Lost target: immediate repair = sing chord 3rd; next action = one decisive attack.
Missed cue: immediate repair = current root; next action = wait next landmark.
Lost bar count: immediate repair = roots only; next action = locate section cadence.
Noisy sweep: immediate repair = single note; next action = resume simpler picking.
Failed displacement: immediate repair = straight quarters; next action = restore grid.
Missed modulation: immediate repair = destination dominant; next action = confirm new tonic.
Lead overruns handoff: immediate repair = stop early; next action = reform comp chord.
Comp misses lead cue: immediate repair = single guide tone; next action = begin lead next beat.
Unknown extra bar: immediate repair = repeat motif; next action = do not invent harmony.
Memory blank: immediate repair = silence + count; next action = enter at next structural bar.

RANDOMIZED SCENARIOS
Verse bar 7: wrong chord: play root on beat 3; correct on next bar; continue through the section exit.
Pre bar 15: missed D7 target: sing F#; land before Gmaj7; continue through the section exit.
Chorus bar 22: pick drops pattern: switch to quarter-note triads; continue through the section exit.
Verse B bar 30: cue arrives early: skip fill; prepare B7; continue through the section exit.
Bridge bar 37: G# missed: play E7 root/third shell; resolve to A; continue through the section exit.
Bridge bar 39: lost count: roots only; identify bar 40 Am; continue through the section exit.
Finale bar 44: displacement fails: return to straight eighths; continue through the section exit.
Final bar: cutoff missed: mute immediately; do not add corrective strum; continue through the section exit.
