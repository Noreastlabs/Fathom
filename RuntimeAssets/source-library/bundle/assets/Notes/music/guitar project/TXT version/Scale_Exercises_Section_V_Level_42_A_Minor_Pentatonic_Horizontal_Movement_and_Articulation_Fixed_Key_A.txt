SCALE EXERCISES - SECTION V, LEVEL 42
A MINOR PENTATONIC HORIZONTAL MOVEMENT AND ARTICULATION - FIXED KEY A

PURPOSE
Turn the five vertical A-minor-pentatonic positions from Level 41 into six horizontal string lanes, then
convert the map into controlled shifts, timed slides, hammer-ons, pull-offs, pitch-matched bends, blue-third
inflection, bend releases, root vibrato, and five position-specific cadence phrases.

FIXED PITCH SET
Notes: A C D E G
Degrees: 1 b3 4 5 b7
Intervals: minor third - whole - whole - minor third - whole
Every fret in Modules A-E belongs to A minor pentatonic. C# appears only as the upper boundary for the
blue-third inflection and is marked as an expressive target, not as a new pentatonic degree.

NOTATION
h = hammer-on | p = pull-off | / = slide upward | \ = slide downward
b(target) = bend to the printed target fret | r = release | ~~~~ = vibrato
b<(target) = blue-third shade: move toward the target but stop below the full half-step pitch

ERROR DIAGNOSIS
NOISY SHIFT: release earlier, move thumb with hand, reduce 4-6 BPM.
FRET FISHING: look at and name the destination before leaving the launch note.
WEAK HAMMER: strike closer to the fret with less travel; do not clamp the thumb.
EMPTY PULL-OFF: keep the destination fretted and use a tiny lateral pluck.
LATE SLIDE: shorten the motion; the destination is a counted tone.
FLAT OR SHARP BEND: compare directly with the fretted target and hold before releasing.
BLUE SHADE REACHES C#: reduce the motion; the full half-step is only the upper boundary.
WIDE VIBRATO: narrow the movement until every pulse returns to center.
STRING NOISE: mute above the bend with the index and below it with the picking hand.
PAIN OR TENSION: stop. Do not force a bend or hold a rigid five-fret stretch.

ADVANCEMENT
Add 2 BPM after three clean runs at the current module length. After two errors, reduce 4-6 BPM or isolate one
lane, seam, bend site, or cadence. Intonation and timing outrank speed. Use clean tone until muting is stable.
