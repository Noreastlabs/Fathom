SCALE EXERCISES - SECTION V, LEVEL 41
A MINOR PENTATONIC FIVE-POSITION MAP AND CONNECTION - FIXED KEY A

PURPOSE
Introduce the genuinely new A minor pentatonic pitch set, keep A as the fixed tonic, and connect five common
positions across the neck. Learn each vertical frame, shift only after the shared anchor at every adjacent-position
seam, then link all five boxes in forward and reverse zigzags.

IMPORTANT PARALLEL-MINOR DISTINCTION
A major pentatonic:  A B C# E F#   degrees 1 2 3 5 6
F# minor pentatonic: F# A B C# E   same notes as A major, re-centered in Levels 39-40
A minor pentatonic:  A C D E G     degrees 1 b3 4 5 b7
A minor pentatonic is the parallel-minor pitch collection. It is not the same fret map with renamed degrees.

A MINOR-PENTATONIC FORMULA
Notes: A C D E G
Degrees: 1 b3 4 5 b7
Intervals: minor third - whole - whole - minor third - whole

POSITION-LABEL NOTE
Pentatonic box numbers vary among methods. This workbook numbers positions in ascending-neck order beginning
with the standard A-minor root frame at fret 5. Fret spans, degree maps, and root locations are authoritative.

POSITION SUMMARY

PICKING AND COUNTS
Each vertical position cell contains 24 notes: 12 ascending and 12 descending with the highest endpoint repeated.
Each shared-anchor seam cell contains 30 notes: six five-note string groups.
The forward zigzag contains 60 notes; the reverse zigzag contains 60 notes; linked circuit = 120 notes.
Five position cells = 120 notes. Four seam cells = 120 notes. Linked zigzag = 120 notes.
Complete Level 41 core map = 360 notes. Five parallel major/minor comparisons add 60 tones. Complete written workout = 420 tones.

STRICT ALTERNATE PICKING
Complete separate D-start and U-start passes. Do not restart at a string crossing, shared anchor, position
boundary, root, outer-string turn, or the forward/reverse turnaround.

1. Play one A-major-pentatonic position from Level 34, then answer in the corresponding A-minor position.

3. Play root-to-root phrases that cross exactly one seam, then two seams, then all four.
4. Start from P5 and descend first.

ERROR DIAGNOSIS
LOST TONIC: Stop on the nearest A root, sing A, and restart the local frame.
WRONG BOX: Name the position span and starting degree before playing.
MAJOR/MINOR CONFUSION: Say A-C-D-E-G and compare it directly with A-B-C#-E-F#.
EARLY SHIFT: Remain in the lower frame through the shared anchor; move only after it sounds.
NOISY SEAM: Release pressure, move the thumb with the hand, and reduce the tempo by 4-6 BPM.
PICK RESET: Mute the strings and air-pick the exact 12-, 30-, 60-, or 120-note count.
TENSION: Stop, shake out, and practice one seam instead of forcing the complete route.

ADVANCEMENT RULES
Add 2 BPM after three clean circuits at the current route length.
After two errors, reduce 4-6 BPM or shorten the route to one adjacent-position seam.
Increase route length before speed when the map or seam is weak.

resolve all ten random-entry cards to the assigned roots; identify all five major/minor color pairs by ear; and
recover the correct position after a deliberate random stop.
