GUITAR EXERCISES - SECTION VII, LEVEL 61
CHORD-TONE SOLOING, CHROMATIC APPROACHES, ENCLOSURES, DELAYED RESOLUTIONS, AND PROGRESSION-SPECIFIC PHRASE CONSTRUCTION

PURPOSE
Convert seventh-chord arpeggios into melodic phrases. Chord tones define destinations; diatonic and chromatic approaches create direction; enclosures frame targets; delayed resolutions create controlled tension; ii-V-I and iiø-V-i phrases preserve progression identity.

CORE FORMULAS
Target first: target-fill-space.
Chromatic approach: target-1 fret -> target or target+1 fret -> target.
Diatonic approach: adjacent key tone -> target.
Enclosure: above-below-target or below-above-target.
Delayed resolution: tension on beat 1, target on the & of 1 or beat 2.
