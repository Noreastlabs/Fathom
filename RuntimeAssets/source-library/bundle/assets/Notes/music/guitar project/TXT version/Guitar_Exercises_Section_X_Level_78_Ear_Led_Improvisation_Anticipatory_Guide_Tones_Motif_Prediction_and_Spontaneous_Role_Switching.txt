FIVE-PASS LADDER
1. Targets only: Sing every beat-one target; play one note; rest for remainder of bar.
2. Accompaniment only: Use roots, shells, or triads. Sing upcoming lead targets even though no lead is played.
3. Printed role map: Follow the comp/lead roles from page 16 exactly. Keep beat 4 available before handoffs.
4. Randomized cues: Partner changes four role handoffs. Player simplifies immediately and preserves the form.
5. Free orchestration: Choose role, register, density, and articulation, but keep the chord and target schedule intact.
