GUITAR EXERCISES - SECTION IX, LEVEL 69
A MELODIC MINOR SCALE CONSTRUCTION, RAISED SIXTH AND SEVENTH CONTROL, TONIC COLORS, AND SEVENTH-CHORD HARMONY

PURPOSE
Introduce A melodic minor by comparing it directly with natural and harmonic minor. Control F/F# and G/G#, train symmetric and classical descending options, develop AmMaj7/Am6 tonic colors, harmonize the scale in triads and seventh chords, and apply the new harmony through progression phrases and a long-form capstone.

THREE A-MINOR FORMS
A natural minor:  A B C D E F  G  A   = 1 2 b3 4 5 b6 b7 1
A harmonic minor: A B C D E F  G# A   = 1 2 b3 4 5 b6 7  1
A melodic minor:  A B C D E F# G# A   = 1 2 b3 4 5 6  7  1

DIRECTIONAL OPTIONS
Symmetric / modern: use A melodic minor ascending and descending.
Classical: ascend with F# and G#; descend through A natural minor with G and F.

COMPLETE WRITTEN WORKOUT: 640 sounding tones.
