SCALE EXERCISES - SECTION V, LEVEL 47
A MAJOR AND A MINOR PENTATONIC MIXING - CHORD-AWARE COLOR CONTROL - FIXED KEY A

PURPOSE
Combine A major pentatonic and A minor pentatonic intentionally without treating their union as a default
eight-note scale. Use A and E as common anchors, control the three color pairs, target the active dominant
chord, preserve the twelve-bar form, and explain every color change after playing it.

PITCH POOLS
A major pentatonic: A B C# E F#    degrees 1 2 3 5 6
A minor pentatonic: A C D E G      degrees 1 b3 4 5 b7
Common tones: A E
Color pairs: C#/C, F#/G, B/D
E7 completion tone: G#* - not a pentatonic member.

COLOR KEY
C = common to both sets; M = major-only; m = minor-only; * = chord-completion tone.

CORE MIXING GRAMMAR
ANCHOR -> COLOR -> CHORD TARGET -> SPACE
Do not run A-B-C-C#-D-E-F#-G as the default exercise. Keep one motif or anchor stable and change one
color decision at a time.
