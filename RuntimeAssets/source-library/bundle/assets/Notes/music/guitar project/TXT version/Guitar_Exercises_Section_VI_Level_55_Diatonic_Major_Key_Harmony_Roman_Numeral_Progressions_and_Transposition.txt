GUITAR EXERCISES - SECTION VI, LEVEL 55
DIATONIC MAJOR-KEY HARMONY, ROMAN-NUMERAL PROGRESSIONS, AND TRANSPOSITION

PURPOSE
Harmonize the major scale, assign the correct triad quality to every degree, group the chords by function, analyze common progression families with Roman numerals, and transpose the same functions through C, G, D, A, E, and F major.

MAJOR-SCALE FORMULA
W-W-H-W-W-W-H

HARMONIZED QUALITY SEQUENCE
I major | ii minor | iii minor | IV major | V major | vi minor | vii° diminished

FUNCTION FAMILIES
Tonic: I, iii, vi
Predominant: ii, IV
Dominant: V, vii°

WRITTEN WORKLOAD
Module A: 42 silent spelling and formation prompts.
Module B: 168 sounded attacks across six harmonized triad ladders.
Modules C-H: 576 attacks across six progression families in six keys.
Module I: 96 nearest-inversion voice-leading attacks.
Module J: 24 written analysis and transposition prompts.
Module K: 96 attacks in the 24-bar circle-of-fifths capstone.
COMPLETE SOUNDED WORKOUT: 936 attacks.
