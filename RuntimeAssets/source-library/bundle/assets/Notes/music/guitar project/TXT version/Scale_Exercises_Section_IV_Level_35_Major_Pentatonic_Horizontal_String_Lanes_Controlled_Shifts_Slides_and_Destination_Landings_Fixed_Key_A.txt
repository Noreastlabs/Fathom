SCALE EXERCISES - SECTION IV, LEVEL 35
A MAJOR PENTATONIC HORIZONTAL STRING LANES - CONTROLLED SHIFTS, SLIDES, AND DESTINATION LANDINGS

PURPOSE
Keep the key fixed as A major pentatonic and turn the five vertical positions from Level 34 into six
horizontal one-string melodies. First relocate silently between three two-note stations, then isolate one
timed slide at each of the four position seams, then land directly on named destinations over increasing
distances. Finish by linking all six strings in a forward and reverse horizontal snake.

IMPORTANT INTERPRETATION
This level does not transpose the shape. Every note belongs to A major pentatonic: A B C# E F#.
The suggested route fingerings are designed for continuous one-string movement and therefore do not always
match the vertical-box fingerings from Level 34. The fret, note, degree, and timing are authoritative.

A MAJOR-PENTATONIC FORMULA
Degrees: 1 2 3 5 6
Notes:   A B C# E F#
Intervals: whole - whole - minor third - whole - minor third

PICKING AND COUNT RULES
Module A: six 12-note picked cells = 72 picked notes.
Module B: 24 slide phrases x 6 sounding tones = 144 sounding tones, but only 96 pick attacks.
Module C: six 16-note destination ladders = 96 picked notes.
Module D: forward 36 + reverse 36 = 72 picked notes.
Complete Level 35 workout = 384 sounding tones / 336 pick attacks.

STRICT ALTERNATE PICKING
Complete separate D-start and U-start passes. Do not restart at a string change, station shift, seam slide,
repeated destination, A root, or forward/reverse turnaround. A slide destination is sounded without a new
pick attack and must arrive exactly on the subdivision.

========================================================================================================================
PRACTICE PROGRESSION
========================================================================================================================
1. Map all six lanes and say notes plus degrees.
2. Play one Module A lane at one note per click.
3. Complete all six silent-shift cells with both pick starts.
4. Add one seam-slide page; count 6 tones but only 4 attacks per string.
5. Complete Module C with finger 1 and no audible relocation.
6. Play the 36-note forward snake without a pause.
7. Play the 36-note reverse snake without a pause.
8. Link the 72-note circuit and accent every A root, then remove the accents.

ERROR DIAGNOSIS
NOISY SHIFT: release earlier, move the thumb with the hand, and reduce 4-6 BPM.
LATE SLIDE: shorten the motion and practice the anchor-to-target pair alone.
FRET FISHING: name and look at the destination before leaving the launch note.
PICK RESET: mute the strings and air-pick the exact 12-, 36-, or 72-note count.
TENSION: stop the full route and return to one string.

ADVANCEMENT RULES
Add 2 BPM after three clean runs at the current route length. After two errors, reduce 4-6 BPM or shorten
the route to one lane or seam. Increase route length before speed when the map is weak. Increase speed
before route length only when every destination is already secure.

MASTERY GATE BEFORE LEVEL 36
Name the six notes and degrees on every string; play all six Module A lanes with D-start and U-start;
execute all 24 seam-slide phrases without an extra pick or late arrival; land on every printed destination
without an audible search; complete the linked 72-note snake on two separate days; identify every A root
by sight and sound; and recover the correct lane after a deliberate random stop.
