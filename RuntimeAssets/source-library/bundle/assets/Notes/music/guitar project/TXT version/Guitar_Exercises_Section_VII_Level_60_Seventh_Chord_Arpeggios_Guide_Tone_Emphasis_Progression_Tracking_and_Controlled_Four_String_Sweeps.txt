ERROR DIAGNOSIS
NOTES OVERLAP: release each fretting finger as the next string sounds.
SWEEP RUSHES: return to one note per click and stop after every string.
QUALITY UNCLEAR: say the seventh and fifth quality before playing.
m7b5 SOUNDS LIKE m7: accent the b5 and compare it to the perfect fifth.
GUIDE TONES HIDDEN: reduce root/fifth volume and accent third/seventh.
PICK RESET: air-pick the complete 8- or 7-note cycle.
TENSION: shorten to two strings and reduce 4-6 BPM.
