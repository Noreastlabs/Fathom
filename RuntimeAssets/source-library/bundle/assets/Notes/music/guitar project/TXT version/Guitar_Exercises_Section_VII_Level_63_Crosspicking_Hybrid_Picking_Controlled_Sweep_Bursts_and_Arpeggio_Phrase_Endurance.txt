GUITAR EXERCISES - SECTION VII, LEVEL 63
CROSSPICKING, HYBRID PICKING, CONTROLLED SWEEP BURSTS, AND ARPEGGIO PHRASE ENDURANCE

PURPOSE
Broaden the arpeggio picking vocabulary while preserving chord identity, time, muting, and musical phrase function. Develop crosspicked rolls, hybrid pick-and-finger patterns, three- and four-string sweep bursts, picking-system comparisons, progression tracking, endurance circuits, and major/minor capstones.

CORE PICKING MAPS
Crosspick roll: low-middle-high-middle-low-middle-high-middle | D-U-D-U-D-U-D-U
Hybrid roll: P-m-a-m-P-m-a-m
Three-string sweep mirror: low-middle-high-high-middle-low | D-D-D-U-U-U
Four-string sweep mirror: low-2-3-high-high-3-2-low | D-D-D-D-U-U-U-U

ENDURANCE, RECOVERY, AND MASTERY
========================================================================================================================
TIMED BLOCKS: 15/15, 30/30, 45/45, then 60/60 seconds. Add duration before tempo.

RECOVERY
Crosspick catches a string: simplify to a three-note roll.
Hybrid finger misses: continue with the pick-only target.
Sweep overlaps: stop on the highest clean note and release.
Pick cycle is lost: use the next target as a new downstroke.
Fatigue appears: insert a full-beat rest and reduce density.

MASTERY GATE
- Complete all crosspicking and hybrid cells with equal tone and both pick starts.
- Execute every sweep burst without note overlap.
- Perform every four-system comparison at one stable tempo.
- Play all twelve progression routes with the assigned systems.
- Complete all four endurance circuits for 30 clean seconds with equal rest.
- Perform both sixteen-bar capstones without losing the key sequence.
