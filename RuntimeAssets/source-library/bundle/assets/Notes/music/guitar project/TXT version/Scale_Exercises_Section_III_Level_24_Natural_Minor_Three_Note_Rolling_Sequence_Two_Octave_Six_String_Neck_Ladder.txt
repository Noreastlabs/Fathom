SCALE EXERCISES - SECTION III, LEVEL 24
NATURAL MINOR THREE-NOTE ROLLING SEQUENCE - TWO-OCTAVE SIX-STRING NECK LADDER: A TO A

PURPOSE
Carry the Level 23 overlapping three-note sequence across the complete two-octave natural-minor frame
from Level 22. Complete the 78-note up/down rolling cell, release pressure, move the entire hand-and-thumb
frame exactly one fret, and repeat through every natural-minor key. Begin with A natural minor at low-E
fret 5, climb to A natural minor at fret 17, then reverse and return one fret at a time.

WHAT CHANGES FROM LEVEL 23
Level 23 isolated the rolling motion inside one octave. Level 24 preserves the same three-note overlap but
extends it through positions 1-15 and all six strings. The new work is not a new sequence formula; it is
maintaining that formula through the middle octave, the G-string backreach, the B-string tuning offset,
the single high-e endpoint, the repeated high root, and the one-fret relocation.

IMPORTANT INTERPRETATION
Moving one fixed scale shape by one fret transposes it. A natural minor is the starting rung, not the key
of every rung. A fixed-key A-natural-minor exercise in every neck position requires changing the note layout
and remains a separate later branch.

NATURAL-MINOR FORMULA
One octave: 1 2 b3 4 5 b6 b7 8
Two octaves: 1 2 b3 4 5 b6 b7 8 9 b10 11 12 b13 b14 15
Intervals: whole - half - whole - whole - half - whole - whole, then repeat
The tablature uses a Pos row for scale positions 1-15; the degree map above identifies the altered tones.

TWO-OCTAVE SHAPE - ROOT FRET R ON LOW E
Low E string: R, R+2, R+3          Fingers 1,3,4
A string:     R, R+2, R+3          Fingers 1,3,4
D string:     R, R+2               Fingers 1,3
G string:     R-1, R, R+2          Fingers 1,2,4
B string:     R, R+1, R+3          Fingers 1,2,4
High e string:R                     Finger 1
The complete map spans R-1 through R+3. Do not hold a static five-fret stretch. Release and rebuild the
frame for the G-string backreach and again for the B-string offset.

ROLLING THREE-NOTE SEQUENCE
UP:   1-2-b3 | 2-b3-4 | b3-4-5 | 4-5-b6 | 5-b6-b7 | b6-b7-8 | b7-8-9 | 8-9-b10 | 9-b10-11 | b10-11-12 | 11-12-b13 | 12-b13-b14 | b13-b14-15
DOWN: 15-b14-b13 | b14-b13-12 | b13-12-11 | 12-11-b10 | 11-b10-9 | b10-9-8 | 9-8-b7 | 8-b7-b6 | b7-b6-5 | b6-5-4 | 5-4-b3 | 4-b3-2 | b3-2-1
Each new triplet begins one scale position farther along. The line moves forward while briefly revisiting
the previous two positions. Do not pause or reset at a triplet boundary.

STRING-CROSSING MAP
UP:   EEE | EEA | EAA | AAA | AAD | ADD | DDG | DGG | GGG | GGB | GBB | BBB | BBe
DOWN: eBB | BBB | BBG | BGG | GGG | GGD | GDD | DDA | DAA | AAA | AAE | AEE | EEE
The route includes no-crossing groups, early crossings, and late crossings. Keep every landing inside the
same triplet pulse, especially at D-to-G and G-to-B where the visible fret geometry changes.

CELL AND PICKING RULE
The complete cell contains 13 ascending triplets and 13 descending triplets: 26 groups and 78 notes.
The final high root of the ascent is immediately followed by the same high root to begin the descent.
Because each triplet is odd, the starting stroke alternates from group to group. Because the complete
78-note cell is even, the next key begins with the original pick orientation.
D-start groups: D-U-D | U-D-U | D-U-D | U-D-U ...
U-start groups: U-D-U | D-U-D | U-D-U | D-U-D ...
Do not restart at a triplet, string crossing, middle octave, repeated high root, or one-fret shift.

NECK ROUTE
UP:   A(5) > Bb(6) > B(7) > C(8) > C#(9) > D(10) > Eb(11) > E(12) > F(13) > F#(14) > G(15) > G#(16) > A(17)
DOWN: A(17) > G#(16) > G(15) > F#(14) > F(13) > E(12) > Eb(11) > D(10) > C#(9) > C(8) > B(7) > Bb(6) > A(5)

PRACTICE RULE
Use eighth-note triplets: one three-note group per beat. Begin 15-20 BPM below your clean Level 23 tempo.
First master the 13 ascending triplets in A natural minor, then the 13 descending triplets. Link them
without restarting the pick cycle and make the two high-root notes equal in length and volume. Next use
2-3 neighboring rungs and pause one beat after each 78-note cell while moving the complete frame one fret.
Repeat with both pick starts. Remove the pause, connect the ladder, reverse after the fret-17 cell, and
return to fret 5. Add 2 BPM after three clean round trips. Reduce 4-6 BPM after two errors, a broken
triplet pulse, a missed backreach or B-string offset, dragged shift noise, or rising tension. Advance after
the complete route is clean with both pick starts on two separate days.
