GUITAR EXERCISES - SECTION VII, LEVEL 62
DIAGONAL ARPEGGIO CONNECTIONS, STRING SKIPPING, POSITION SHIFTS, AND FULL-NECK CHORD-TONE MOTIF DEVELOPMENT

PURPOSE
Connect triad and seventh-chord fragments across the full neck while preserving chord identity, target timing, pick continuity, and muting. Develop string skipping, silent position shifts, inversion starts, major and minor progression routes, long-form motifs, recovery, and full-neck capstones.

CORE CONNECTION RULES
1. Name the destination note, role, string, and fret before moving.
2. Release excess pressure before a silent shift.
3. Move thumb and hand together.
4. A skipped string receives no accidental pick contact.
5. Strict alternate picking is the default; economy motion is limited to adjacent strings.
6. Preserve at least one motif identity: rhythm, role schedule, contour, or register relationship.

ERROR DIAGNOSIS
SKIPPED STRING RINGS: reduce pick depth and mute with both hands.
SHIFT ARRIVES LATE: preview the target and move during the previous duration.
CHORD IDENTITY LOST: return to four chord tones inside one position.
PICK CYCLE RESETS: air-pick through the shift before sounding it.
MOTIF DISAPPEARS: preserve rhythm or role schedule; change one variable only.
REGISTER JUMP SOUNDS RANDOM: connect the regions through a common role or repeated target.

- Transform the seed motif in eight recognizable ways.
