SCALE EXERCISES - SECTION V, LEVEL 45
A BLUES SHUFFLE RHYTHM, TRIPLET PLACEMENT, AND TWELVE-BAR FORM - FIXED KEY A

PURPOSE
Move the resolution vocabulary from Level 44 into time. Train straight-versus-shuffle spacing,
triplet-subdivision placement, silent bars and partial-bar rests, pickups into chord changes,
stop-time phrasing, twelve-bar navigation, turnaround entry, and reduced-click performance.
The objective is to make the shuffle and the form audible even when only one note is played.

CORE PITCH POOL
A blues scale: A C D Eb E G
Degrees:       1 b3 4 b5 5 b7

MARKED SUPPORT NOTES
C# = 3*       Major-third or turnaround destination.
F# = 6*       Turnaround support note.
F  = b6*      Chromatic lower approach to E.
B  = 2*/E7-5 Fifth of E7 where explicitly marked.
G# = 7*/E7-3 Third of E7 where explicitly marked.
An asterisk means the pitch has a temporary job; it is not a new permanent member of the A-blues scale.

========================================================================================================================
LEVEL 45 CAPSTONE - THREE-CHORUS RHYTHM CIRCUIT
========================================================================================================================
Use a click on beats 2 and 4. Do not stop between choruses.

CHORUS 1
One root on beat 1 of every bar. 12 attacks. Bar 12 = E7 turnaround.

CHORUS 2
Root-shuffle chorus from Module H. 48 attacks. Bar 12 = E7 turnaround.

CHORUS 3
Model rhythm chorus from Module H. 48 attacks. Replace bar 12 with an A-tonic closing phrase if desired.

The capstone reuses previous material and is not added to the 540-attack written workload.

RECORDING REVIEW
- Can the listener identify bars 5 and 9?
- Do shuffle pairs remain equal across chord changes?
- Are long rests complete rather than shortened?
- Does chorus 3 sound denser without becoming faster?
- Does the final A sound closed rather than accidental?

========================================================================================================================
PRACTICE PROGRESSION
========================================================================================================================
1. Clap the straight and shuffle grids.
2. Complete all eight single-note placement cards.
3. Play the three twelve-bar root passes.
4. Land every pickup on the new bar.
5. Preserve all six rest cards.
6. Link the three call-and-response studies.
7. Complete the stop-time chain.
8. Perform the landmark, root-shuffle, and model choruses.
9. Execute all six turnaround entries.
10. Complete the click-displacement passes and the three-chorus capstone.

ERROR DIAGNOSIS
RUSHED SHUFFLE: return to one note per click and count the silent middle subdivision.
SHORT REST: mute the strings and clap the complete two-bar mask.
LATE PICKUP: loop only beat 4 into the next downbeat.
LOST FORM: use roots only and speak every bar number.
EARLY TURNAROUND: count bars 9-10-11 before playing the line.
CLICK DEPENDENCE: reduce route length before reducing click frequency.

ADVANCEMENT RULES
Add 2 BPM after three clean repetitions of the current module.
After two errors, reduce 4-6 BPM or shorten the exercise to four bars.
Increase route length before speed when bar numbers are uncertain.
Increase speed before click reduction when the form is secure but the hands are slow.
Stop after any pain, persistent tension, or uncontrolled bend motion.

MASTERY GATE BEFORE LEVEL 46
Perform the three straight/shuffle pattern pairs without changing tempo; complete all eight single-note
placement cards; play all three twelve-bar root passes; land nine pickups on beat 1; preserve six rest
studies; link three call-and-response studies; execute four stop-time studies; complete the landmark,
root-shuffle, and model choruses; play all six turnaround entries; finish all five click-displacement
passes; and complete the three-chorus capstone on two separate days.
