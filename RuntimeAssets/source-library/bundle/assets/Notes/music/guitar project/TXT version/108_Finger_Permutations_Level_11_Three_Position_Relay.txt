108 FINGER PERMUTATIONS - LEVEL 11: THREE-POSITION RELAY
Our lesson plans go to 11.
1+2+1 double-shift control on one string at a time in standard tuning.
P5 mapping: 1=index/fret 5, 2=middle/fret 6, 3=ring/fret 7, 4=pinky/fret 8.
P7 mapping: 1=index/fret 7, 2=middle/fret 8, 3=ring/fret 9, 4=pinky/fret 10.
P9 mapping: 1=index/fret 9, 2=middle/fret 10, 3=ring/fret 11, 4=pinky/fret 12.

MOVEMENT RULE
Divide every four-digit code 1+2+1 across three position frames.
SHIFT UP: play note 1 in P5, release pressure and move the entire hand and thumb to P7, play notes 2-3,
then release and move to P9 for note 4. Repeat one string at a time in this order: E>A>D>G>B>e.
SHIFT DOWN: play note 1 in P9, move the whole frame to P7 for notes 2-3, then move to P5 for note 4.
Repeat one string at a time in this order: e>B>G>D>A>E.
The first landing note is note 2. The second landing note is note 4.
The four-digit finger code never changes, and both frame relocations are mandatory.

WHY THIS IS LEVEL 11
Levels 8-10 exhausted the useful single-shift boundaries: after a complete code, after note 2, after note 3,
and after note 1. Level 11 is the horizontal equivalent of the earlier three-part relay levels. It places two
whole-frame relocations inside one four-note cell while keeping the pick on a single string. This closes the
isolated position-shifting section before string changes and position changes are combined.

OVERLAPPING-FRET RULE
The direction labels describe the hand frame, not necessarily the melodic contour. A landing pitch may rise,
repeat, or fall. Overlapping fret numbers do not cancel a move. Release, relocate, and use the finger assigned
by the printed position frame rather than stretching or substituting a different fingering.

PICKING RULE
Downstroke start: D | U-D | U.
Upstroke start:   U | D-U | D.
Both barlines mark fretting-hand shifts only. Do not restart, reverse, or exaggerate the alternate-picking cycle.

PRACTICE RULE
Use one 12-exercise set per session. Begin 10-15 BPM below the slower of your two clean Level 10 modes.
Pass 1: use even eighth notes, pause one subdivision at both shift barlines, and lightly accent notes 2 and 4.
Pass 2: remove both pauses while keeping the accents, then remove the accents and equalize all four notes.
Convert the connected cell to straight sixteenth notes. Play SHIFT UP and SHIFT DOWN with a downstroke start,
then repeat both routes with an upstroke start.
After three clean loops, add 2 BPM next session. After two late landings, slides, squeaks, errors, or rising tension,
reduce 4-6 BPM. Advance after the full set is clean in both directions and both pick starts on two separate days.
Then restore the existing triplet and quintuplet pulses without moving either code-bound shift point to match the beat.

AFTER MASTERY
Keep the 1+2+1 grouping and vary only the position distance. Examples: P5>P8>P11 or P5>P7>P10, plus their
descending equivalents. These are distance variants of Level 11, not another full 108-exercise level.
