ERROR DIAGNOSIS
NOTES RING TOGETHER: release each string after its note.
ARPEGGIO LOSES QUALITY: say 1-3-5 or 1-b3-5.
INVERSION UNKNOWN: name the bass role before playing.
ECONOMY BECOMES STRUM: separate attacks and reduce pick depth.
ALT PICK RESET: air-pick the complete six- or five-note count.
STRING SKIP MISSES: look at the destination string before leaving.
SCALE HIDES CHORD: accent triad tones, then reduce scale notes.

MASTERY GATE
Form and spell all 48 silent commands; play all 48 blocked-plus-arpeggio shapes; demonstrate alternate and economy picking without note overlap; complete all inversion cycles, quality switches, permutation cells, scale connections, progression cells, and call-response pairs; meet the standard on two separate days.
