SCALE EXERCISES - SECTION IV, LEVEL 36
A MAJOR PENTATONIC EXPRESSIVE ARTICULATION - LEGATO, SLIDES, BENDS, RELEASES, AND VIBRATO
FIXED KEY A - FIVE POSITIONS - FRETS 4-17

PURPOSE
Turn the fixed-key A-major-pentatonic map from Levels 34-35 into controlled expressive motion. The pitch map
does not change. What changes is how each pitch begins, connects, bends, releases, and sustains. Train hammer-ons
and pull-offs in all five positions, combine legato with the four shared-anchor seams, calibrate whole-step bends
against named target frets, add pre-bend releases and pitch-centered vibrato, then finish with five short cadences.

IMPORTANT INTERPRETATION
Expression does not excuse inaccurate rhythm or pitch. A hammer-on, pull-off, slide arrival, bend target, release,
or vibrato pulse is a timed musical event. Picked and unpicked notes must remain equal in volume and duration.
All core bends in this level are whole-step bends to a note two frets higher. The minor-third gaps 3->5 and 6->1
would require a one-and-a-half-step bend and are deliberately excluded from the core bend module.

A MAJOR PENTATONIC
Notes: A B C# E F#
Degrees: 1 2 3 5 6

ARTICULATION NOTATION
h = hammer-on; p = pull-off; / = ascending slide; \ = descending slide; b(T) = bend to target fret T;
r = release; pb(T) = pre-bend silently to target pitch before the attack; ~ = vibrato.

COUNTS
Module A: 5 position cells x 24 sounding tones = 120 tones; 12 attacks per cell = 60 attacks.
Module B: 4 seams x 6 strings x 5 tones = 120 tones; one attack per phrase = 24 attacks.
Module C: 9 whole-step bend sites x 4 attacks = 36 attacks.
Module D: 6 pre-bend cells x 3 attacks = 18 attacks; 8 A-root vibrato holds = 8 attacks.
Module E: 5 cadence phrases = 19 attacks; articulation creates the remaining pitch motion.
Complete core workout: 165 pick attacks, plus 240 explicitly counted legato/slide tones, 15 bend or pre-bend
cells, 8 root-vibrato holds, and 5 cadence phrases.

========================================================================================================================
PRACTICE PROGRESSION
========================================================================================================================
1. Map the notation and say the A-major-pentatonic notes and degrees before playing.
2. Complete one Position 1 legato cell at one sounding tone per click.
3. Play all five Module A cells with both D-start and U-start attack cycles.
4. Add one seam page; keep all five tones equal from a single attack.
5. Calibrate one bend site by alternating the fretted target and bent target.
6. Complete all nine bend cells without overshooting, sagging, or adjacent-string noise.
7. Add pre-bend releases, then the eight A-root vibrato holds.
8. Play the five cadences separately, then link them without extra attacks.

ERROR DIAGNOSIS
WEAK HAMMER: strike closer to the fret with less travel; do not squeeze harder with the thumb.
EMPTY PULL-OFF: keep the destination fretted and use a tiny lateral pluck rather than simply lifting.
LATE SLIDE: shorten the motion; the arrival fret is a counted tone, not transportation.
FLAT BEND: support the bending finger, rotate the wrist, and compare directly with the target fret.
SHARP BEND: stop pushing after the target; hold the pitch before adding vibrato.
WIDE VIBRATO: reduce the motion until every pulse returns to the pitch center.
STRING NOISE: mute above the bend with the fretting index and below it with the picking hand.
PAIN OR TENSION: stop. Do not force the whole-step bend or substitute a one-and-a-half-step bend.

ADVANCEMENT RULES
Add 2 BPM after three clean runs at the current module length. After two errors, reduce 4-6 BPM or isolate one
string, seam, bend, or cadence. Intonation outranks speed. Use a clean tone until the pitch and muting are stable.

MASTERY GATE BEFORE LEVEL 37
Play all five legato cells with equal picked and unpicked volume; execute all 24 one-pick seam chains without an
extra attack; match all nine whole-step bends to their fretted targets; perform six pre-bend releases without a
scoop; hold all eight A roots with controlled vibrato; link the five cadence phrases on two separate days; and
identify the note and degree of every bend source, bend target, and final root.
