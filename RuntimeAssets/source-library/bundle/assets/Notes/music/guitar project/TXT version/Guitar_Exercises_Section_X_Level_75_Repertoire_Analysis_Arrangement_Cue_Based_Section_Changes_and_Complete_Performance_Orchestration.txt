GUITAR EXERCISES - SECTION X, LEVEL 75
REPERTOIRE ANALYSIS, ARRANGEMENT, CUE-BASED SECTION CHANGES, AND COMPLETE-PERFORMANCE ORCHESTRATION

COMPLETE WRITTEN WORKLOAD: 1368 notated contacts plus one complete 60-bar performance.

PURPOSE
Move from exercise execution to complete-performance behavior: analyze form, assign roles and textures, respond to cues, place fills inside available space, change register and dynamics by section, recover locally, and finish the full form without restarting.

MODULE A - FORM AND HARMONIC ANALYSIS
96 contacts. Reduce sections to roots, guide tones, then complete voicings. Mark dominants, repeated sections, and high-risk transitions.

MODULE B - ARRANGEMENT ROLES AND TEXTURE
128 contacts. Practice foundation, comping, counterline, lead, and silence roles. Change density and register without changing tempo.

MODULE C - CUE MECHANICS
96 contacts. Visual, spoken-count, musical, and chart cues. Receive the cue while continuing to play; change exactly at the destination barline.

MODULE D - FILL PLACEMENT AND SECTION CONTRAST
128 contacts. Begin fills on beats 1, 2, 3, 4, & of 3, and & of 4. Every fill must end before the next structural attack.

MODULE E - DYNAMIC AND REGISTER ORCHESTRATION
160 contacts. Practice p-mp-mf-f contours, register ladders, and the complete arrangement map.

MODULE F - TRANSITION RISK AND INTERNAL TIME
120 contacts. Loop the last bar of the old section and first bar of the new section. Reduce click support from every quarter to section starts only.

MODULE G - REHEARSAL LADDER AND ROLE HANDOFFS
240 contacts. Speak chart, roots, shells, written textures, cues/fills, dynamics/register, role changes, then one uninterrupted take.

MODULE H - MASTER FORM PERFORMANCE MAP
160 contacts. Perform the complete 60-bar arrangement in section-sized blocks before the uninterrupted take.

MODULE I - RECOVERY AND CAPSTONE
240 contacts. Inject wrong notes, wrong chords, missed cues, lost subdivisions, and fill overruns. Preserve pulse and rejoin at the earliest safe landmark.

RECOVERY ORDER
1. Keep pulse.
2. Find active bar.
3. Play root or shell.
4. Find next section landmark.
5. Restore texture.
6. Review only after the take ends.

CAPSTONE STANDARD
One count-in. One complete 60-bar take. Score 0-2 for form/bar count, chord function, rhythmic continuity, cue response, texture contrast, dynamic shape, role handoff, recovery, and ending/release. Pass at 15/18 or better on two separate days, with no zero in form, cue response, rhythmic continuity, or recovery.
