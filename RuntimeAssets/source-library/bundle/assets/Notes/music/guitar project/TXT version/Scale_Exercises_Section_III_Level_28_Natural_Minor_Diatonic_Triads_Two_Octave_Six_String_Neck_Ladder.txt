SCALE EXERCISES - SECTION III, LEVEL 28
NATURAL MINOR DIATONIC TRIADS - TWO-OCTAVE SIX-STRING NECK LADDER
==============================================================================================================================================

PURPOSE
Level 28 stacks one more scale tone onto the diatonic-thirds idea from Level 27. Every group now plays
a root, third, and fifth: 1-b3-5, 2-4-b6, b3-5-b7, and onward. The exercise converts natural
minor into a connected chain of broken triads while retaining the same two-octave six-string shape
and one-fret chromatic neck route.

WHAT CHANGES FROM LEVEL 27
Level 27 isolated two-note thirds and their major/minor qualities. Level 28 adds the diatonic fifth,
turning every interval pair into a complete triad. The mechanical demand becomes a three-note group
that may span one, two, or three strings; the musical demand is to hear and name i, ii diminished,
III, iv, v, VI, and VII without losing the continuous triplet pulse.

NATURAL-MINOR FORMULA
One octave: 1 2 b3 4 5 b6 b7 8
Two octaves: 1 2 b3 4 5 b6 b7 8 9 b10 11 12 b13 b14 15
Intervals: whole - half - whole - whole - half - whole - whole, then repeat

MAIN SEQUENCE
Ascending:  1-b3-5 | 2-4-b6 | b3-5-b7 | 4-b6-8 | 5-b7-9 | b6-8-b10 | b7-9-11 | 8-b10-12 | 9-11-b13 | b10-12-b14 | 11-b13-15
Descending: 15-b13-11 | b14-12-b10 | b13-11-9 | 12-b10-8 | 11-9-b7 | b10-8-b6 | 9-b7-5 | 8-b6-4 | b7-5-b3 | b6-4-2 | 5-b3-1
Each new triad begins one scale position farther forward. The line climbs a third and another third,
then rolls back three scale positions to begin the next chord. Do not pause or reset at the rollback.

HARMONIC FUNCTION AND QUALITY
Ascending:  i minor | ii diminished | III major | iv minor | v minor | VI major | VII major | i minor | ii diminished | III major | iv minor
Descending: iv minor | III major | ii diminished | i minor | VII major | VI major | v minor | iv minor | III major | ii diminished | i minor
Natural minor has a minor v and a major VII because degree b7 is not raised. Harmonic minor will alter
those functions later; do not import the raised seventh into this workbook.

TWO-OCTAVE SIX-STRING SHAPE - ROOT FRET R ON LOW E
Low E string: R, R+2, R+3          Fingers 1,3,4        Positions 1,2,b3
A string:     R, R+2, R+3          Fingers 1,3,4        Positions 4,5,b6
D string:     R, R+2               Fingers 1,3          Positions b7,8
G string:     R-1, R, R+2          Fingers 1,2,4        Positions 9,b10,11
B string:     R, R+1, R+3          Fingers 1,2,4        Positions 12,b13,b14
High e:       R                    Finger 1             Position 15
The complete map spans R-1 through R+3. Release and rebuild for the G-string backreach and again for
the B-string offset; do not hold a static five-fret stretch.

STRING-PATH MAP
Ascending:  EEA | EAA | EAD | AAD | ADG | ADG | DGG | DGB | GGB | GBB | GBe
Descending: eBG | BBG | BGG | BGD | GGD | GDA | GDA | DAA | DAE | AAE | AEE
Some triads remain on two strings, while others span three strings. Keep the middle note rhythmically
centered and every landing equal in volume through the D-G and G-B transitions.

CELL DESIGN
- 11 ascending triads / 33 notes
- repeat the high root at the turnaround as the first note of the descending line
- 11 descending triads / 33 notes
- 22 triads / 66 notes total per key
- shift the entire hand-and-thumb frame one fret only after the final 5-b3-1 triad

PICKING
Downstroke start: D-U-D | U-D-U | D-U-D | U-D-U ... through all 22 triads.
Upstroke start:   U-D-U | D-U-D | U-D-U | D-U-D ... through all 22 triads.
Every three-note triad flips the starting stroke of the next triad. The complete 66-note cell is even,
so every new key begins on the same pick orientation. Do not reset at group boundaries, string changes,
the middle octave, the repeated high root, or a new key.

RHYTHM
Learning pulse: one triad per beat as eighth-note triplets.
Performance pulse: two triads per beat as sixteenth-note triplets (sextuplets).
Accent the root of each triad while learning, then remove the accents and equalize every note.

PRACTICE PROGRESSION
1. A natural minor, first octave only: i through iv, then reverse.
2. A natural minor, all 11 ascending triads.
3. A natural minor, all 11 descending triads.
4. Link the 66-note cell; articulate both high-root notes without resetting.
5. Use two or three neighboring keys with a one-beat pause only after each complete cell.
6. Remove the pause and complete the entire route with both pick starts.

CHROMATIC ROUTE
Up:   A -> Bb -> B -> C -> C# -> D -> Eb -> E -> F -> F# -> G -> G# -> A
Down: G# -> G -> F# -> F -> E -> Eb -> D -> C# -> C -> B -> Bb -> A
Do not replay the high A-natural-minor rung during the down pass unless beginning a new round.
