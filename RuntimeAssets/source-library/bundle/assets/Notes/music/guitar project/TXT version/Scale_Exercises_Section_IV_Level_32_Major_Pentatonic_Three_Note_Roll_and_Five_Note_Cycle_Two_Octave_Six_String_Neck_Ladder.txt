SCALE EXERCISES - SECTION IV, LEVEL 32
MAJOR PENTATONIC THREE-NOTE ROLL AND FIVE-NOTE CYCLE - TWO-OCTAVE SIX-STRING NECK LADDER: A TO A

PURPOSE
Keep the Level 31 two-octave major-pentatonic frame and reorganize its eleven ordered scale positions
into two overlapping odd-count sequence modules. Practice each module separately, then link both modules
before moving the complete hand-and-thumb frame exactly one fret. Begin with A major pentatonic at low-E
fret 5, climb to the octave A at fret 17, then reverse and return one fret at a time.

MAJOR-PENTATONIC FORMULA
Degrees per octave: 1 2 3 5 6 8
Intervals: whole - whole - minor third - whole - minor third
The complete two-octave position line is: 1 2 3 5 6 8 9 10 12 13 15.

TWO-OCTAVE SIX-STRING SHAPE - ROOT FRET R ON LOW E
Low E string: R, R+2               Fingers 2,4
A string:     R-1, R+2             Fingers 1,4
D string:     R-1, R+2             Fingers 1,4
G string:     R-1, R+1             Fingers 1,3
B string:     R, R+2               Fingers 2,4
High e string: R                   Finger 2
The four-fret frame runs from R-1 through R+2. Do not change the Level 31 fingering.

MODULE A - THREE-NOTE ROLL
UP:   1-2-3 | 2-3-5 | 3-5-6 | 5-6-8 | 6-8-9 | 8-9-10 | 9-10-12 | 10-12-13 | 12-13-15
DOWN: 15-13-12 | 13-12-10 | 12-10-9 | 10-9-8 | 9-8-6 | 8-6-5 | 6-5-3 | 5-3-2 | 3-2-1
Each new triplet begins one pentatonic position farther along. The module contains 18 triplets and 54 notes.
Native grouped pulse: one eighth-note triplet per beat.
String paths up:   EEA | EAA | AAD | ADD | DDG | DGG | GGB | GBB | BBe
String paths down: eBB | BBG | BGG | GGD | GDD | DDA | DAA | AAE | AEE

MODULE B - FIVE-NOTE CYCLE
UP:   1-2-3-5-6 | 2-3-5-6-8 | 3-5-6-8-9 | 5-6-8-9-10 | 6-8-9-10-12 | 8-9-10-12-13 | 9-10-12-13-15
DOWN: 15-13-12-10-9 | 13-12-10-9-8 | 12-10-9-8-6 | 10-9-8-6-5 | 9-8-6-5-3 | 8-6-5-3-2 | 6-5-3-2-1
Each group contains five consecutive pentatonic positions and restarts one position farther along.
The module contains 14 quintuplets and 70 notes. Native grouped pulse: one quintuplet per beat.
String paths up:   EEAAD | EAADD | AADDG | ADDGG | DDGGB | DGGBB | GGBBe
String paths down: eBBGG | BBGGD | BGGDD | GGDDA | GDDAA | DDAAE | DAAEE

PICKING AND CELL RULE
Every three-note or five-note group is odd, so the starting stroke reverses at the next group.
Module A D-start: D-U-D | U-D-U | D-U-D ...
Module B D-start: D-U-D-U-D | U-D-U-D-U | D-U-D-U-D ...
Use the mirrored patterns for an upstroke start. Module A has 54 notes and Module B has 70 notes; both
totals are even. Therefore Module B and every new key begin with the original pick orientation.
Do not reset the pick at a group, string crossing, middle root, high-root turn, module boundary, or shift.

RHYTHM MODES
Grouped mode: one triplet or one quintuplet per beat. Learn this version first.
Displacement mode: play continuous sixteenth notes and let the three- or five-note group accent migrate
across 4/4. Treat displacement as a variation only after the grouped pulse is secure.

NECK ROUTE
UP:   A(5) > Bb(6) > B(7) > C(8) > Db(9) > D(10) > Eb(11) > E(12) > F(13) > Gb(14) > G(15) > Ab(16) > A(17)
DOWN: A(17) > Ab(16) > G(15) > Gb(14) > F(13) > E(12) > Eb(11) > D(10) > Db(9) > C(8) > B(7) > Bb(6) > A(5)

COUNTS
Module A: 54 notes / 18 beats per key; 25-cell round trip = 1,350 notes.
Module B: 70 notes / 14 beats per key; 25-cell round trip = 1,750 notes.
Linked A+B: 124 notes / 32 beats per key; 25-cell round trip = 3,100 notes / 800 beats.

AUDIT AND REPAIR
Lost group: speak the degree window aloud and loop only the two groups surrounding the error.
Rushed subdivision: return to one note per click and rebuild equal notes without a first-note accent.
Crossing accent: accent the landing deliberately, then remove it while preserving the movement.
Pick reset: mute the strings and air-pick the complete module through the failing boundary.
Shift scrape: release pressure before moving, move the thumb with the frame, and reduce 4-6 BPM.

FULL ROUND-TRIP CHALLENGE
1. Start with A major pentatonic at low-E fret 5.
2. Play linked Module A + Module B through rungs 01-13 without restarting the pick cycle.
3. Reverse after the fret-17 card and return through rungs 12-01.

5. Repeat the route with the opposite starting stroke.
