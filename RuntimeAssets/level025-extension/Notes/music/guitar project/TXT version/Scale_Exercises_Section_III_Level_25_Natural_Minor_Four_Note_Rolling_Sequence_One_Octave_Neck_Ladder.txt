SCALE EXERCISES - SECTION III, LEVEL 25
NATURAL MINOR FOUR-NOTE ROLLING SEQUENCE - ONE-OCTAVE NECK LADDER: A TO A

PURPOSE
Turn the compact one-octave natural-minor scale into overlapping four-note groups. Complete the full
40-note up/down rolling cell, release pressure, move the entire hand-and-thumb frame exactly one fret,
and repeat through every natural-minor key. Begin with A natural minor at low-E fret 5, climb to A natural
minor at fret 17, then reverse and return one fret at a time.

WHY THIS LEVEL RETURNS TO ONE OCTAVE
Level 24 expanded the three-note rolling sequence across two octaves and all six strings. Level 25
deliberately returns to the compact three-string octave so the new four-note overlap can be learned without
simultaneously managing the upper-octave backreach and B-string offset. Level 26 will extend this exact
sequence across the complete two-octave frame.

IMPORTANT INTERPRETATION
Moving one fixed scale shape by one fret transposes it. A natural minor is the starting rung, not the key of every rung.
A fixed-key A-natural-minor exercise in every neck position requires changing the note layout and remains a separate branch.

NATURAL-MINOR FORMULA
Degrees:   1 2 b3 4 5 b6 b7 8
Intervals: whole - half - whole - whole - half - whole - whole

COMPACT ONE-OCTAVE SHAPE - ROOT FRET R ON LOW E
Low E string: R, R+2, R+3          Fingers 1,3,4
A string:     R, R+2, R+3          Fingers 1,3,4
D string:     R, R+2               Fingers 1,3
The four-fret hand frame runs from R through R+3.
Ascending notes per string: 3-3-2. Descending notes per string: 2-3-3.

ROLLING FOUR-NOTE SEQUENCE
UP:   1-2-b3-4 | 2-b3-4-5 | b3-4-5-b6 | 4-5-b6-b7 | 5-b6-b7-8
DOWN: 8-b7-b6-5 | b7-b6-5-4 | b6-5-4-b3 | 5-4-b3-2 | 4-b3-2-1
Each new group begins one scale degree farther along. At every group boundary, the line drops a diatonic
third and then continues forward. Three notes overlap from one group to the next. Do not pause, accent,
or reset at a group boundary.

STRING-CROSSING MAP
UP:   EEEA | EEAA | EAAA | AAAD | AADD
DOWN: DDAA | DAAA | AAAE | AAEE | AEEE
The crossings move from late to balanced to early as the line travels across the three-string frame.
Keep the first note after every crossing equal in volume and time.

CELL AND PICKING RULE
The complete up/down cell contains 10 groups and 40 notes. The octave root is repeated at the turn:
the last note of 5-b6-b7-8 is followed by the first note of 8-b7-b6-5. Because 40 is even, the next
key begins with the same pick orientation. Because every group has four notes, every group also begins
with the same stroke.
D-start groups: D-U-D-U | D-U-D-U | D-U-D-U ... through all 10 groups.
U-start groups: U-D-U-D | U-D-U-D | U-D-U-D ... through all 10 groups.
Never restart the pick cycle at a new group, string crossing, scale turn, or one-fret shift.

NECK ROUTE
UP:   A(5) > Bb(6) > B(7) > C(8) > C#(9) > D(10) > Eb(11) > E(12) > F(13) > F#(14) > G(15) > G#(16) > A(17)
DOWN: A(17) > G#(16) > G(15) > F#(14) > F(13) > E(12) > Eb(11) > D(10) > C#(9) > C(8) > B(7) > Bb(6) > A(5)

PRACTICE RULE
Use sixteenth notes: one four-note group per beat. Begin at roughly 75 percent of your clean Level 24
metronome setting; for example, if Level 24 is clean at 80 BPM, begin near 60 BPM. Master the A-natural-
minor cell alone, then use 2-3 neighboring rungs and pause one beat after each 40-note cell. During the
pause, release pressure and move the complete frame one fret. Repeat with both pick starts. Then remove
the pause, connect the ladder, reverse at fret 17, and return to fret 5. Add 2 BPM after three clean round
trips. Reduce 4-6 BPM after two errors, an uneven group pulse, dragged shift noise, a crossing accent,
or rising tension. Advance after the full route is clean with both pick starts on two separate days.

2. Play all 13 cells without restarting the alternate-picking cycle.
3. Reverse after the fret-17 A-natural-minor cell and return through rungs 12-01.
4. Name each key and say 1-2-b3-4-5-b6-b7 before its first low root.
5. Repeat the complete route with the opposite starting stroke.
One complete route contains 25 cells x 40 notes = 1,000 picked notes.

CLEAN-ROUND STANDARD
No missing or added group; no pause or accent at a group boundary; no broken sixteenth-note pulse; no
wrong scale tone; no open string; no scrape or tempo change at the one-fret relocation; no accent caused
by an early, balanced, or late crossing; and equal volume on both repeated octave-root notes at the turn.

AFTER THE BASIC LADDER IS CLEAN
1. Accent note 1 of every group, then remove all group accents.
2. Repeat four passes, moving the accent to notes 2, 3, and 4.
3. Accent only scale degrees 1, b3, 5, and b7 wherever they occur.
4. Begin selected sessions at fret 17 and descend first.
5. Choose a random root fret from 5-17, name the key, and continue in either direction.
These are practice variations, not separate full levels.
