GUITAR EXERCISES - SECTION X, LEVEL 77
INTERVAL RECOGNITION, CHORD-FUNCTION HEARING, SUNG GUIDE TONES, MELODIC DICTATION, CALL-AND-RESPONSE, AND IMMEDIATE FRETBOARD REALIZATION

PURPOSE
Hear -> label -> sing -> locate silently -> play one decisive attack -> verify -> transfer.

NO-SEARCH RULE
After the target is sung, use one silent setup and one attack. Diagnose a wrong attack; do not slide around until the note is found.

MODULE F - IMMEDIATE REALIZATION
Practice named scale degrees and chord tones in low, middle, and high registers with one silent setup and one attack.

MODULE G - TIMED REACTION
Begin at 4 seconds. Reduce to 3 seconds and then 2 seconds only after >=90% accuracy.

MODULE H - CAPSTONE
Reconstruct an unfamiliar 16-bar excerpt by form, bass, function, rhythm, contour, guide tones, exact notes, and immediate fretboard realization.

MASTERY
Intervals >=90%; sing guide tones through major and minor cadences; transcribe eight short melodies; echo eight calls after a one-bar delay; realize named degrees/chord tones without searching; pass the 16-bar capstone on two separate days.

Level 78: ear-led improvisation, anticipatory guide tones, motif prediction, and spontaneous accompaniment/lead role switching.
