PURPOSE
Turn the harmonic-minor mode and chord-function material from Level 67 into a dominant-resolution and improvisation system. Develop E Phrygian dominant, E7b9, the G#dim7 upper structure, minor-third diminished symmetry, controlled b9/b13 and borrowed #9 colors, cadence phrases, call and response, recovery, and a long-form capstone.
