SCALE EXERCISES - SECTION II, LEVEL 18
MAJOR SCALE DIATONIC THIRDS - TWO-OCTAVE SIX-STRING NECK LADDER
==========================================================================================================================

PURPOSE
Level 18 leaves stepwise scale motion and begins intervallic sequencing. Each pair skips one scale degree:
1-3, 2-4, 3-5, and onward. After each upper note, the next pair rolls back one degree and continues upward.
The complete exercise retains the same two-octave six-string major-scale shape and one-fret chromatic neck route
used in Levels 13, 15, and 17.

MAIN SEQUENCE
Ascending:  1-3 | 2-4 | 3-5 | 4-6 | 5-7 | 6-8 | 7-9 | 8-10 | 9-11 | 10-12 | 11-13 | 12-14 | 13-15
Descending: 15-13 | 14-12 | 13-11 | 12-10 | 11-9 | 10-8 | 9-7 | 8-6 | 7-5 | 6-4 | 5-3 | 4-2 | 3-1

INTERVAL-QUALITY PATTERN
Ascending:  M3 | m3 | m3 | M3 | M3 | m3 | m3 | M3 | m3 | m3 | M3 | M3 | m3
Each pair is either a major third (M3 = 4 semitones) or minor third (m3 = 3 semitones).
The quality is determined by the major key, not by a fixed fret shape.

CELL DESIGN
- 13 ascending pairs / 26 notes
- repeat the high root at the turnaround
- 13 descending pairs / 26 notes
- 52 notes total per key
- shift the entire hand-and-thumb frame one fret only after the final 3-1 pair

PICKING
Downstroke start: D-U | D-U | D-U | ... through all 26 pairs.
Upstroke start:   U-D | U-D | U-D | ... through all 26 pairs.
Because every pair and the complete 52-note cell are even, every pair and every new key begins on the same
pick orientation. Do not reset at string changes, the middle octave, the repeated high root, or a new key.

RHYTHM
Learning pulse: one pair per beat as eighth notes.
Performance pulse: two pairs per beat as straight sixteenth notes.
Accent the first note of each pair while learning, then remove the accents and equalize all notes.

PRACTICE PROGRESSION
1. A major, first octave only: pairs 1-3 through 6-8, then reverse.
2. A major, all 13 ascending pairs.
3. A major, all 13 descending pairs.
4. Link the 52-note cell; articulate both high-root notes without resetting.
5. Use two or three neighboring keys with a one-beat pause only after each complete cell.
6. Remove the pause and complete the entire route with both pick starts.

CHROMATIC ROUTE
Up:   A -> Bb -> B -> C -> Db -> D -> Eb -> E -> F -> Gb -> G -> Ab -> A
Down: Ab -> G -> Gb -> F -> E -> Eb -> D -> Db -> C -> B -> Bb -> A
Do not replay the high A-major rung during the down pass unless beginning a new round.

FULL ROUND-TRIP CHALLENGE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Start at A major on low-E fret 5. Complete rungs 01-13 without restarting alternate picking. Reverse after
the fret-17 A-major cell and return through rungs 12-01. Say each key name aloud before its first low root.
Repeat the entire route with the opposite starting pick stroke.

Route count: 25 cells x 52 notes = 1,300 picked notes.
Tempo rule: add 2 BPM after three clean trips; reduce 4-6 BPM after two errors or signs of tension.

CLEAN-ROUND STANDARD
- no missing, doubled, reversed, or reordered pair
- no pause or accent during the one-degree rollback
- correct string changes, B-string offset, and assigned fingering
- both high-root notes at the turn are clearly articulated and equal in volume
- no scrape or tempo change during a one-fret shift
- major and minor third qualities remain audible inside one connected line

VARIATIONS AFTER THE BASIC LADDER IS CLEAN
1. Accent the first note of every pair, then remove all pair accents.
2. Accent only the upper note of every pair without rushing the rollback.
3. Say M3 or m3 before each pair in one selected key.
4. Play one pair per beat, then two pairs per beat at the same metronome marking.
5. Begin selected sessions at fret 17 and descend first.
6. Choose a random root fret from 5-17, name the key, and continue either direction.

and both pick starts on two separate days.
