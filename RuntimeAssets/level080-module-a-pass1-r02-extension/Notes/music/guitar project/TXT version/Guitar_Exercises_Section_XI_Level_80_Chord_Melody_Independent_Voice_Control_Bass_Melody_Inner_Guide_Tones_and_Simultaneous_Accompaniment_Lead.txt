Guitar Exercises - Section XI, Level 80
CHORD MELODY, INDEPENDENT VOICE CONTROL, BASS, MELODY, INNER GUIDE TONES, AND SIMULTANEOUS ACCOMPANIMENT/LEAD

PURPOSE
Carry melody, harmony, bass motion, and inner guide-tone movement at the same time without losing pulse, form, or melodic priority.

VOICE PRIORITY
1. top melody
2. bass
3. guide tones
4. extensions / color

MODULE A - MELODY ON TOP
Cmaj7: melody E-F-G-E; route x-5-5-3; top line ascends then resolves.
Dm7: melody F-G-A-F; route 6-8-10-6; keep D bass stable.
G7: melody B-C-D-B; route 12-13-15-12; hear 3-11-5-3.
Am7: melody C-D-E-C; route 13-15-17-13; minor-third identity.
Fmaj7: melody A-B-C-A; route 10-12-13-10; 3-#11?-5-3; treat B as passing color.
Cmaj7: melody G-A-B-G; route 8-10-12-8; 5-6-7-5.

MODULE B - BASS INDEPENDENCE
Cmaj7: bass C-B-A-G; harmony Cmaj7 -> Cmaj7/B -> Am7 -> G7.
Am7: bass A-G-F-E; harmony Am7 -> G7 -> Fmaj7 -> E7.
Dm7: bass D-C-B-A; harmony Dm7 -> Cmaj7 -> Bm7b5 -> Am7.
G7: bass G-F-E-D; harmony G7 -> Fmaj7 -> Em7 -> Dm7.
C major walkup: bass C-D-E-F; harmony Cmaj7 -> Dm7 -> Em7 -> Fmaj7.
A minor walkup: bass A-B-C-D; harmony Am7 -> Bm7b5 -> Cmaj7 -> Dm7.

MODULE C - INNER GUIDE TONES
Cmaj7 -> Am7: voice1 B -> C; voice2 E -> E; bass C -> A.
Dm7 -> G7: voice1 C -> B; voice2 F -> F; bass D -> G.
G7 -> Cmaj7: voice1 F -> E; voice2 B -> B; bass G -> C.
Bm7b5 -> E7: voice1 A -> G#; voice2 D -> D; bass B -> E.
E7 -> Am7: voice1 D -> C; voice2 G# -> G; bass E -> A.
Fmaj7 -> G7: voice1 E -> F; voice2 A -> B; bass F -> G.

MODULE D - FOUR-VOICE DROP AND RELEASE
Six coordination cells: all voices, melody-only insertions, bass-only insertions, dyad call/response, build-from-fragments, and clean cutoff.

MODULE G - SIMULTANEOUS ACCOMPANIMENT AND LEAD
Bass + melody: P - m - P - a -; thumb/pick bass, finger melody.
Shell + melody: P m a m | P m a m; shell sustains under line.
Chord hit + answer: ALL - - M | - M - -; short chord then top-line response.
Pedal bass: P m P a | P m P a; repeat root under melody.
Inner pedal: P m a m; hold guide tone, move top.
Broken chord: P i m a; low to high arpeggio.
Reverse broken chord: a m i P; high to low answer.
Syncopated melody: P - m a | - m - a; melody avoids bass accents.

MODULE J - RECOVERY
Melody hidden: remove extension; then keep melody + bass.
Bass late: remove inner voices; then play bass + melody only.
Chord change noisy: use shell voicing; then restore full grip later.
Top note missed: play melody alone next beat; then resume harmony after target.
Inner note sustains wrongly: release chord body; then let melody continue.
Right hand confused: return to bass-melody alternation; then rebuild pattern.
Substitution sounds wrong: restore original chord; then keep melody fixed.
Form lost: roots + melody target; then re-enter at cadence.

MASTERY
Pass the 16-bar capstone at 14/16 or better on two separate days, with no zero in melody clarity, bass independence, pulse/form, or recovery.
