108 FINGER PERMUTATIONS - LEVEL 10: ODD-COUNT POSITION TRANSFER
3+1 / 1+3 shift-point control on one string at a time in standard tuning.
P5 mapping: 1=index/fret 5, 2=middle/fret 6, 3=ring/fret 7, 4=pinky/fret 8.
P7 mapping: 1=index/fret 7, 2=middle/fret 8, 3=ring/fret 9, 4=pinky/fret 10.

MOVEMENT RULE
Mode A - 3+1 late shift: play digits 1-3 in the source position, release pressure, move the whole hand and thumb,
then play digit 4 in the destination position. The landing note is note 4.
Mode B - 1+3 early shift: play digit 1 in the source position, shift the whole frame, then play digits 2-4
in the destination position. The landing note is note 2.
SHIFT UP moves P5 to P7 and repeats one string at a time in this order: E>A>D>G>B>e.
SHIFT DOWN moves P7 to P5 and repeats one string at a time in this order: e>B>G>D>A>E.
The four-digit finger code never changes. The position direction describes the hand frame, not the melodic interval.
A landing note may rise, repeat, or fall in pitch. Overlapping fret numbers still require a real frame relocation.

WHY THIS IS LEVEL 10
Level 9 used a balanced 2+2 split, so each side of the shift contained two picked notes. Level 10 moves the shift
to an odd-count boundary. The fretting-hand release and landing now occur after either one note or three notes,
while strict alternate picking continues without a reset. String crossing remains removed so the new challenge is isolated.

PICKING RULE
Mode A: downstroke start = D-U-D | U; upstroke start = U-D-U | D.
Mode B: downstroke start = D | U-D-U; upstroke start = U | D-U-D.
The barline marks the position shift only. It does not restart, reverse, or otherwise alter the alternate-picking cycle.

PRACTICE RULE
Use one 12-exercise set and one mode per session. Begin 8-12 BPM below your clean Level 9 continuous tempo.
Pass 1: play even eighth notes, pause one subdivision at the shift barline, and lightly accent the landing note.
Pass 2: remove the pause and accent, then connect the cell as straight eighths and finally straight 16ths.
Play SHIFT UP and SHIFT DOWN with a downstroke start, then repeat both routes with an upstroke start.
After three clean loops, add 2 BPM next session. After two late landings, slides, squeaks, errors, or rising tension,
reduce 4-6 BPM. Advance after both modes are clean in both directions on two separate days.
Then restore the existing triplet and quintuplet pulses without moving the code-bound shift point to match the beat.
