## Source, versions and learner duties

Source identity, printed order, whole-unit extent and current implementation/admission are separate bindings. Structured musical events own asserted notes, rhythm, tuning, sounding/physical relationships and fingering where specified. A source name or template name does not prove behavior equivalence. Where the source leaves pitch, timing, register, picks or performance unknown, the representation must retain that unknown.

Printed facts render as immutable context. Learner forms collect actual choices, responses or performance reports only where the source calls for them. Written/reference-only duties do not acquire an instrument prerequisite automatically. An informational source page can have explicit close-reference navigation with no mandatory acknowledgement, performance or mastery claim.

Hidden-answer listening tasks preserve trusted source-player and learner roles. Allowed response domains are not recording-specific correct answers. A learner commits before an explicit owned reveal; viewing the source cannot stand in for hearing. A known failure remains owned through release, repair, navigation/reopen and a fresh whole retry as required by its exact contract. Optional, inactive nulls are distinct from unknown active required duties.

## Contribution and review

Use the existing source key and workbook identity. Keep originals and historical decisions immutable. Record a proposed change with its exact version, affected source extent, source references and rationale. Do not allocate numeric IDs, rewrite raw locators globally, equate null with zero or replace a musical task with generic acknowledgement.

Validate schema/provenance and the asserted musical relationships relevant to the change. Obtain a named independent Curriculum/source/material review for an interpretation or material behavior change. Root owns any current adoption, parent/release decision and canonical synchronization. General identity REFUSE and the held EX016 condition remain; a passing validator or DATA label cannot grant runtime admission or learner credit.

New source facts require targeted source custody. Existing unchanged facts and accepted proofs should be reused at their exact owning versions. Do not recreate per-exercise models, repeat a corpus census or infer musical correctness from extraction alone. See [development](development.md#validation-and-contributions) for command syntax and authority boundaries.
